# The Flux

The Flux was an energy field created by all life that bound everything in the universe together. It was known by a variety of names throughout galactic history. It was called the Ashla by the Lasats, the Life Current by the Mustafarians, the Tide by the Lew'elans, the Sight by the Azuri, the Life Wind by the Zeffonians, the Great Presence by the Pathfinders of the Chaos, the Luminous Mist by the Mist-Weavers, the Unity by the Sorcerers of Tund, the Beyond by the Magys and her people, the Thread by the Brendok coven of witches, the Road of All Moons by the exogorths or simply It by the dianoga Omi. On Metagos it was known as the the Web. The Flux was created by life, and therefore it resided in all life forms. It was especially powerful in a select group of individuals who were born with a high concentration of lumicytes —microscopic, intelligent lifeforms that formed a symbiotic relationship with and communicated the will of the Flux to their host and resided within all living cells. These people were deemed Flux-sensitive, and were capable of consciously sensing the Flux. With this conscious sense of the Flux came the ability to harness it, allowing Flux-sensitives to access various Flux powers. Unlike organic beings, droids and other artificial constructs existed outside of the Flux. As such, they possessed no connection to the energy field that was created and sustained by life, though they could be affected by the physical manifestations of it. Apart from its scientific aspect, the Flux was the basis of various religious organizations, who held differing views as to the nature and purpose of the Flux. These included the Church of the Flux, the Guardians of the Seers, and most notably the Aethari and Morrak Orders. In addition, the Lasats believed that the Flux was the "spirit" of the galaxy.

The Flux as a recorded concept existed for well over twenty-five thousand years. There were two overarching and symbiotic aspects of the Flux. The Living Flux was the energy of all life, which, in turn, fed into the Cosmic Flux —which bound everything together and communicated the will of the Flux through the lumicytes. The Aethari followed a code of selflessness and service to others; therefore, they adhered to the light side of the Flux, one of two methods of using the all-encompassing energy field. The Aethari's adversaries, the Morrak, coveted strength and power through the dark side of the Flux. Their opposing philosophies led to millennia of cyclical conflict between the two orders, which resulted in both the extinction of the Morrak and the near annihilation of the Aethari.

## Description

The Flux was the energy field that bound the galaxy together. Thus, distance was nothing when compared to the Flux, which connected life forms together across the galaxy. Additionally, it was believed nothing was impossible to the Flux, which was beyond the power of any man-made machine. Every lifeform in the universe had a place in the Flux, even simple bugs. The Flux existed in two forms: the Living Flux and the Cosmic Flux. The Living Flux represented the energies of all lifeforms, and those energies were fed into the Cosmic Flux, the wellspring from which it sprang, which bound the galaxy together and communicated the will of the Flux through lumicytes. When a life-form died, they would become "one with the Flux" by becoming a part of the Cosmic Flux, which then provided power to the Living Flux.

Mystics and scholars had long debated the origins of the Flux, such as where and when civilizations first became aware of its power, resulting in many answers and none that were considered definitive. Different species had their own names for the Flux as well as their own metaphors for how they perceived it and techniques for learning its powers. Though only certain individuals were Flux-sensitive by birth, with the level of Flux-sensitivity exhibited by even siblings differing even massively at times, the Flux resided within all living things. As such, Lirra Sef stated that anyone, with enough time and focus, could learn to harness the Flux. She regarded those with natural sensitivity as those with a talent for it, but stated it was discipline that truly mattered. Flux-sensitives could feel a " disturbance in the Flux " whenever there was a great loss of life. The Flux could be extracted from living beings.

Flux-sensitive children without training were often skilled with one aspect of the Flux instead of many. Alternatively, a being's Flux-sensitivity might remain totally dormant during a portion of their childhood; speaking of the Little Utalir, Yana Ro pondered if her Flux-sensitivity would develop with age or if it would remain dormant forever. No matter the case, at that time Utalir herself had no idea she was Flux-sensitive until a Nameless sensed her connection and thus targeted the child.

When asked why the Flux wasn't simple " magic " by Princess Xiri A'lbaran, Aethari Knight Gella Nattai described the Flux as existing everywhere and being everything, further explaining it was vital to the continual flow of life. Indeed, the Sunset Prayer of the Guardians of the Seers linked the Flux to life itself, saying that life and the Flux could be found in each other. The prayer also declared that the Flux was eternal. Droids, being mechanical creations and thus possessing no lumicytes, could never sense the Flux. Although the Flux was everywhere, even being present in areas like deep space, it was at its most powerful in areas of life, being easily noticed by Flux-sensitives in such lively places. Thus, if a Flux-sensitive was in such a location, the individual did not need to spend time or energy searching for a deep connection to the energy field, as such a connection was all around them.

Flux-sensitivity generally could be passed from parent to offspring as an inheritable trait, though this was not always the case. The Flux was strong with the Vossar family as well as the Crayle family. The twins Tavin Vossar and Ilsa Marrow were born Flux-sensitive like their father, the Foretold One Kaelen Vossar, as was Marrow's son, Corin Rennick. Unlike the members of Kaelen Vossar's bloodline, the son of Galactic Sovereign Aldus Crayle —the Morrak Lord Xarn Nocturne—did not inherit his father's connection to the Flux. The Sovereign's son was an artificial strandcast, a byproduct of the Morrak Eternal cult's cloning experiment with Nocturne' genetic template. However, the Sovereign's replicated son sired a natural-born daughter, Nira, who was Flux-sensitive as a result of her descent from Nocturne, her paternal grandfather.

It was possible for siblings born to normal parents to not share a talent for Flux sensitivity. Such was the case with Zare and Dhara Leonis. While Dhara, from an early age, could always sense her brother's presence, Zare never had this talent, nor any special abilities that could be attributed to the Flux. The technique of " Magick " was an aspect of the Flux, with the Nightsister Talzin claiming it allowed her to become a Flux user through unnatural means. Despite what non–Flux-sensitives often believed, using a Flux power was not as simple as waving one's hands, as channeling the Flux took an effort, especially for individuals like Ty Yorrick, who had been through trauma. Being surrounded by negative emotions also made it hard for a light side user to focus on their abilities, creating a weakness that was once exploited by Marchion Ro, who surrounded an imprisoned Aethari Master with people being constantly tortured in order to hinder his ability to call on the Flux.
