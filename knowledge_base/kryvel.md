# Kryvel

Kryvel was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name, located in the Velisk sector of the galaxy's Far Reach Territories. It was the homeworld to the primitive frostmaw species.

It notably hosted Frostpoint Base during the Sundering War, the temporary headquarters of the League to Restore the Concord, until the Dominion of Astra located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Kryvel. In the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the Striders destroyed.

In the months after the Battle of Sylvar, rumors emerged about the New Concord intending to repurpose Kryvel into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as Chancellor Ysa Talvorn had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Kryvel, where it became the site of another battle during the Iron Blockade.

## Description

From space, the planet of Kryvel looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Kryvel and its sun, while an asteroid belt surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface. Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains. Despite the characterization of Kryvel as being a planet of frozen tundras and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist Kell Tolkani said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit, another third of the planet was covered by oceans.

The temperature, although always frigid, was known to drop to-60°C come nightfall. Although devoid of intelligent life, Kryvel was home to fifteen sub species of snowstriders, which were commonly used by Insurgents as a means of transport, and which Dace Rennick cut open to use as shelter to keep Tavin Vossar and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as frostmaws. During his stay on Kryvel, Vossar was captured by a frostmaw and was hung upside down in the frostmaw's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the power of the Flux, Vossar managed to get his hands on his lumeblade and cut himself down from the roof and sever the frostmaw's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."

## History

### The Free League

By the time of the Sundering War between the Dominion of Astra and the League to Restore the Concord, Kryvel was undergoing an ice age. Various smuggling rings tried to make use of Kryvel as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions. Sometime following the Battle of Ostara, Saponza's Gang, itself a rebel cell, established a base on Kryvel. Kryvel was first surveyed for use as a rebel base by Corporal Purpruff. Jan Harlow, a general in the League to Restore the Concord, listed Kryvel among his preliminary scans for possible League bases. With Kryvel a world so obscure it was not on most star maps despite being extremely close to the Dravosi Trade Spine, it looked to be a perfect spot for a rebel base. Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Kryvel sat on a lesser used hyperlane, and the asteroid field around Kryvel provided an extra degree of protection. That field hampered travel through the Kryvel system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.

In 1 AVC, with more resources at their command than the various smuggling rings that had failed to turn Kryvel into a headquarters, Kryvel was chosen by the rebels as the location for their base of operations. The rebellion then spent two years constructing Frostpoint Base on the planet. With the League's resources at their disposal, rebel engineers worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years. Around 2 AVC, after Harlow was killed in the Battle of Mako-Ta, Ysa Talvorn gave his list of possible bases to Ilsa Marrow after the mission to Shu-Torun, requesting that her team scout the locations. Dace Rennick expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Tavin Vossar responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Insurgency's new home.

### Battle of Kryvel and aftermath

Kryvel was the arena of a battle between the Dominion of Astra and the Free League, which had installed its temporary headquarters, Frostpoint Base, on the ice planet. However, thanks to one of the thousands of probe droids they had dispatched throughout the galaxy, the Dominion loyalists had discovered the League base within a month of the Insurgents' habitation on the planet. Although Frostpoint Base was evacuated in the eleventh hour, the Battle of Kryvel resulted in a clear defeat for the League after the elite Dominion Blizzard Flux overran it.

In the months following the Battle of Kryvel, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the Striders destroyed by the Free League during the Dominion's attack. Following the Battle of Sylvar, Kryvel became the site of another battle, occurring during the Iron Blockade imposed upon the Velisk sector.

### New Concord Era

In 5 AVC, Troithe Acting Governor Fara Yadeez heard rumors that the New Concord intended to repurpose Kryvel into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion. Major Soran Keize, however, dismissed the credibility of the rumor as Concord Chancellor Ysa Talvorn had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment, and that repurposing Kryvel, where the Insurgency had been badly defeated by the Dominion, had too much poetry.

By the New Concord Era, Kryvel returned to obscurity, the battlefields being picked apart and long-abandoned. The cruise ship Starfarer of Hyperlight Tours visited Kryvel as part of their galactic history themed tours during which they landed and showed the passengers the battlefield. By 28 AVC, Valor Quay, the son of the Starfarer ' s capatin Hansifred Quay, led a group of of younger male away from the tour without permission and showed them into the ruins of an abandoned Strider. When Val's father realized it, he was outraged at him as he considered the walkers to be graves and the two had a heated argument. Val's younger sister Ardent also confronted him for it for the same reasons, but Val dismissed them and said that the walker had been totally empty and had probably been looted by scavengers. After the arguments, Val Quay left the ship to live on his own. At some point between then and 31 AVC, the Dominion's successor state the Iron Mandate took control of Kryvel, and Hyperlight Tours stopped visiting the planet as Captain Quay didn't considered it to be safe anymore. Kryvel was mentioned in the Galactic Explorer's Guide, a survival guide written by Rondo Ohnaka and DK-RA-43 by 34 AVC where it was described as a "footnote" in galactic history.
