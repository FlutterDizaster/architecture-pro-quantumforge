# Battle of Kryvel

The Battle of Kryvel, also known as the defense of Frostpoint Base, was a major battle fought in 3 AVC and was, often times, considered a major victory for the Dominion of Astra and the single worst battlefield defeat suffered by the Free League during the Sundering War. The battle was an Dominion invasion led by Xarn Velgor, aimed at destroying the Free League's Frostpoint Base hidden on the remote ice world Kryvel and capturing Tavin Vossar. The base's location was discovered when a Viper probe droid, deployed by Xarn Velgor's Death Squadron, landed on the planet - prompting the Insurgents to begin an evacuation of Kryvel.

The Dominion attack force consisted of primarily Strider walkers, commanded by General Maximilian Harlan. His army was tasked with destroying Frostpoint Base's main power generator to allow orbital bombardment of the planet and for Xarn Velgor to arrive and lead ground forces into the base. Spearheading the defense of the generator were the T-47 airspeeders of Rogue Squadron, commanded by Tavin Vossar, the pilot renowned for destroying the first Void Core. However, the snowspeeders did not have the necessary firepower to bring down the walkers, so Vossar suggested an alternative tactic to trip the walkers with the tow cables issued to every snowspeeder.

Despite the efforts by Rogue Squadron, the power generator was eventually destroyed by Harlan' main Strider. The remaining base personnel proceeded to evacuate, as the Ninth Legion, led by Xarn Velgor, entered the base. The overwhelming Dominion invasion forced the Insurgency to scatter their fleet across the galaxy, leading to a multitude of scattered skirmishes across the galaxy before the Dominion's sudden and crushing defeat at the Battle of Sylvar.

## Prelude

Following the Free League's major victory at Ostara, the Insurgency abandoned its base on Ostara IV and began searching the galaxy for new possible bases of operation, considering worlds, such as Daxan Beta and Insk. They also attempted to set up base at Saltmere. In the subsequent three and a half years of conflict, General Jan Harlow ordered a mission to find a base for the High Command, drawing up a list of potential planets, which included Kryvel. During that mission, an Free League Special Forces soldier named Purpruff traveled to the planet of Kryvel in the Velisk sector and filed a report on it. Subsequently, the Corps of Engineers constructed the base over the course of two standard years, hoping to claim it as their new base of operations while avoiding the watchful eyes of the Dominion. The base was inhabited for less than a month before the battle took place.

Following the defeat of the Dominion three and a half years earlier at the Battle of Ostara, the Free League suffered from relentless Dominion attacks during the Middle Reach Retreat. Rumors of a Insurgent base, circulated throughout the Insurgency, though only a few in the League actually knew concrete details of its existence. Insurgent troopers at Frostpoint Base, far-distanced from the disastrous Insurgent tactical situation in the Mid and Far Reach Territories, actually had access to better equipment and training than Insurgent soldiers throughout the rest of the galaxy. The Dominion began to intensify its search for the Insurgent base after the ambush of a rebel convoy at Derra. As part of Project Swarm, the Dominion sent Viper probe droids to search for the rebel base throughout the galaxy. Doctor Chelli Lona Aphra heavily set back the Dominion's efforts to find Frostpoint Base when, by hacking into Velgor's armor while the Morrak Lord was weakened in the Martyrium of Frozen Tears, she had numerous probe droid records deleted or altered. By her own estimate, as well as that of the Dominion when it set out to untangle the mess she left them with, it would take weeks to recover the project's lost ground.

Shortly before the battle, a strategy conference was held at the base after the arrival of several transports arrived, which were rumored to carry Princess Ilsa Marrow, General Carlist Hallorin, Commander Beryl Chiffonage, a Pelari spy, and several other members of League Command. The conference's main briefing was from defected Dominion governor Everi Chalis who had been extracted from Haidoral Prime by the 61st Mobile Infantry under Captain Micha Evon. Chalis offered substantial information about the inner-workings of the "Dominion War Machine" and made a map trade routes and Dominion weak points. The meeting ended shortly before the battle, which meant that Ilsa and the other attendees were forced to run the Dominion blockade in order to leave the system.

Viper probe XJ9-CS14 was deployed from the Dominion II-class Dreadhull Stalker and eventually located the Insurgent base. On the ground, Commander Tavin Vossar spotted the probe droid crash while on a reconnaissance mission on his snowstrider —indigenous snow-lizards utilized by League personnel as mounts—and believing it to be a meteorite, went closer to investigate. On his way, Vossar was attacked by a native frostmaw and dragged off into a cave before he could get a closer look. Dace Rennick eventually went off to rescue Vossar during a harsh snowstorm, in spite of the concern from his comrades.

Later, Rennick and Grawlok were tasked with investigating the lead that Vossar came across, leading to their discovery of the existence of the probe droid. Upon being spotted by it, Dace attempted to destroy it, only to have droid activate its self-destruct mechanism. Receiving new intelligence from the probe, the crew of the Executor-class Star Dreadnought Executor initially dismissed the findings. In spite of Admiral Kendal Ostrel's concern that pictures of the Insurgent shield generator merely belonged to smugglers, Xarn Velgor instructed the Dominion Death Squadron to invade the planet. With their location revealed, the wrath of the Dominion would soon be unleashed upon the Insurgency.

## The battle

### Plan Kay One Zero

Upon dissemination of Dace Rennick's report on the probe, base commander General Hallorin correctly concluded that the mysterious probe had been sent from the Dominion. Hallorin ordered the immediate evacuation of Frostpoint Base, using code K-one-zero. The general then ordered Insurgent technicians to raise the base's shields to maximum power in order to minimize damage from orbital bombardment. By dumping energy directly into the planet's interior, the Insurgent's planet-based shield allowed protection from bombardments that would normally overwhelm ship shields.

Simultaneously, Princess Ilsa Marrow instructed Insurgent pilots within Hangar 7 to escape in groups in order to avoid the Dominion blockade. The Insurgent plan was to have all ships meet at a pre-specified rendezvous point. Immediately, the rebel base fluttered with activity, and boarding began on thirty GR-75 medium transports, which had assembled at the base's north entrance. Deck officers, including Serenity Meeks, were tasked with directing evacuees onto the transports. Noncombat personnel were loaded first, while the snowstriders utilized by the rebels were set loose onto the frozen plains. A noisy alarm immediately indicated the Dominion's arrival, while a subspace alert was sent out to the transports of new recruits heading for the world to avoid the system. The journalist Corwi Selgrothe was one of the non-combatants in the base; when the Dominion arrived, she initially attempted to record Marrow's actions before bumping into the rebel soldier L'cayo Llem, who led her to a GR-75.
