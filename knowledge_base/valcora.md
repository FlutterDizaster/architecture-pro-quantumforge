# Valcora

Valcora (pronounced / 'kɔɹəsɑnt /), also known as Jewel of the Heart Worlds, or Dominion Center during the Dominion Era, was an ecumenopolis planet located in the Valcora system of the Valcora subsector in the Heart Worlds's Corusca sector that served as the throneworld of the both the Concord of Worlds and the Dominion of Astra that replaced it. Prior to being covered by over five thousand metallic levels of what would become known as Dominion City, Valcora was the ancient homeworld of the Taung and Zhell species. Though debated by historians, the theory that Valcora was the ancestral world of humans remained unproven. Noted for its cosmopolitan culture and towering skyscrapers, Valcora's population consisted of trillions of citizens hailing from a vast array of both humanoid and alien species. In addition, Valcora's strategic location at the end of several major trade routes enabled it to grow in power and influence, causing the city-planet to surpass its early rivals and become the hub of galactic culture, education, finance, fine arts, politics and technology. It was the location of several major landmarks, including the Aethari Temple, Monument Plaza, and the Assembly Building.

Serving as the capital of the galaxy since the ancient days of the old Concord of Worlds, Valcora was at the center of many historic events, such as the Aethari Order's conflict with the Morrak. Although the planet fell under Morrak control for a time, Concord forces ultimately liberated Valcora with the aid of the Aethari Knights. Their victory was so decisive that at one point the Morrak became all but extinct, replacing an age of darkness and strife with the democratic rule of the modern Concord of Worlds. Retaining its status as galactic capital, it was on Valcora where the Galactic Assembly governed the galaxy for centuries, establishing an era of peace that lasted for almost a millennium under the protection of the Aethari peacekeepers. However, political intrigue wrought by corruption and secession transformed Valcora into a dangerous environment by the time of the Mirror Wars with the Coalition of Free Systems. On the eve of the New Order, fulfilling a grand scheme, Supreme Chancellor Aldus Crayle, who was, in fact, the Morrak Lord Xarn Nocturne, declared the Aethari enemies of the state, resulting in a galaxy-wide purge that all but destroyed the Aethari Order. Without the Aethari to oppose him, Crayle dissolved the Concord and declared himself Galactic Sovereign to the applause of the now Dominion Assembly.

Valcora continued to serve as galactic capital under the Dominion, having become the Sovereign's throneworld, although Crayle himself disappeared from public view for the most part, leaving the day-to-day governing to his advisors in the Dominion Ruling Council. The Dominion maintained a firm hold on Valcora throughout the Sovereign's reign, but with his defeat at the Battle of Sylvar in 4 AVC by the League to Restore the Concord, citizens of Valcora celebrated by revolting. Unable to command the military's allegiance as the rebels restored the Concord, Grand Vizier Tor Ostrak's rule was limited to Valcora while Fleet Admiral Gallius Vorn, the self-proclaimed Counselor to the Dominion, assumed de facto control of the collapsing Dominion government. Hoping to escape prosecution for war crimes, Ostrak ultimately surrendered himself to the New Concord in return for amnesty. Following Vorn's death at the Battle of Saddar in 5 AVC, Ostrak signed the Galactic Concordance —a peace treaty recognizing the Dominion's capitulation and the end of the Sundering War. Ostrak himself was permitted to return to Valcora as the head of a provisional government. Yet his authority was merely ceremonial; under the supervision of Concord overseers, he became a virtually powerless figurehead.

Unlike previous galactic governments, the New Concord broke with millennia of tradition by not establishing Valcora as their headquarters, instead preferring to designate other member worlds with the status of galactic capital through an electoral system. During the New Concord Era, Valcora had become the capital of the New Concord as it was designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure. As part of the New Concord Assembly, Valcora enjoyed full senatorial representation and, by the time of the Cold War, was aligned with the worlds that identified as Centrists. However, by this point, Valcora had actually fallen under the control of crime syndicates. Now a symbol of the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current era, gangs fought a bloody war for control by the time after the Battle of Saltmere.

During the Iron Mandate-Ember Front War, at least two sizeable battlegroup were deployed by the Iron Mandate to Valcora in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from the ecumenopolis as the Dominion of Astra once did, however these efforts were defeated. In the aftermath of the Battle of Umbrath, various worlds sought to drive the Iron Mandate military from their soil. Valcora was one such world where an uprising took place as part of a galaxy-wide effort to overthrow the Iron Mandate.

## Description

### Overview

Valcora, also known as Dominion Center during the Dominion Era or as the Jewel of the Heart Worlds, was an ecumenopolis Planet located within the Valcora system in the Valcora subsector of the Corusca sector in an intersecting portion of the Heart Worlds and The Interior. The homeworld of the Zhell and Taung species and widely accepted as the origin point of humans, Valcora, located in grid square L-9 of the Standard Galactic Grid, was situated at the junction between many vital and important slipspace routes including the Dravosi Run, the Koros Trunk Line, the Metellos Trade Route, the Namadii Corridor, the Perlemian Trade Route, the Stassia Alley, and the secret Nexus Route, served as the throneworld of multiple Galactic Governments, first for the Concord of Worlds, then for it's successor state, the Dominion of Astra, and even briefly for the New Concord in 9 AVC, leading to the planet gaining the coordinates 0, 0, 0 despite not being in the actual Center of The galaxy. Due to Courscant's importance in galactic history, even later governments such as the Iron Mandate would even attempt to conquer the planet and make it their capital, though their efforts would ultimately fail.

Valcora had four moons — Centax-1, Centax-2, Centax-3, and Hesperidium. Valcora orbited Valcora Prime, the only star of the Valcora system, with an orbital period of 365 Local days, with each day lasting 24 Standard hours. The Valcora week lasted five days, with each days with the name of each day relating to Valcora is some way— Primeday, named after Valcora's sun, Centaxday, named after Valcora's primary moon, Taungsday, named after the native Taung species, Zhellday, named after the native Zhell species, and Benduday, named after the Dai Bendu monks from Valcora. When the need for a standardized time system between worlds arose in the Galaxy, the Galactic Standard Calendar was created, which standardized days and years to Valcora's orbital period and rotational period respectively.
