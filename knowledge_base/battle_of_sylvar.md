# Battle of Sylvar

The Battle of Sylvar, also known as the Mission to Sylvar, the assault on the second Void Core, or the attack on the second Void Core, was a major and decisive battle of the Sundering War, one in which the League to Restore the Concord shattered the rule of the Dominion of Astra and saw the destruction of the VC-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and the deaths of Xarn Nocturne and Xarn Velgor. The triumph of the League over the Dominion, and the void left in the wake of the Sovereign's demise, gave the League the opportunity to give rise to the New Concord, which ultimately prevailed over the Dominion in the Battle of Saddar one year later. The legacy of the Battle of Sylvar reverberated for decades after the fighting ended, including into the brewing war between the Dominion-inspired Iron Mandate and the Ember Front that arose to combat it.

The origins of the Battle of Sylvar emerged from the discovery that the Dominion was using the Forest Moon of Sylvar as a staging ground to construct a second Void Core, the first of which had been destroyed four years earlier during the Battle of Ostara. Pelari spies employed by the League gained the intelligence needed to determine the Void Core's location and learn that the Sovereign planned to personally oversee the final phases of construction, though the League was unaware that this was an elaborate plot to destroy Tavin Vossar, a Aethari and the son of Xarn Velgor, and the League itself once and for all. The battle began after the League Fleet assembled near Sullust, and special operations ground forces were deployed to Sylvar to disable the shield protecting the armored battle station.

The League Fleet soon arrived to discover the Dominion Navy waiting for them, along with a fully operational Void Core. Allied with the native Nubbits, ground forces under the command of General Dace Rennick and Princess Ilsa Marrow destroyed the shield, allowing the League Fleet to attack the superweapon. Meanwhile, Vossar engaged Velgor in a final lumeblade duel aboard the Void Core. After defeating his father, the Sovereign betrayed Velgor by attempting to convince the Aethari Knight to kill Velgor and turn to the dark side of the Flux, only for Tavin to refuse. To save his son from the wrath of the Sovereign, Velgor surprised and betrayed Nocturne, killing the Dark Lord of the Morrak, and sacrificing his own life in the process—an act that fulfilled the Aethari prophecy of the Foretold One and brought balance to the Flux. Shortly thereafter, the Free League forces destroyed the Void Core. Dominion forces were left in tatters, enabling the League to form the New Concord and forge a new future free of the Morrak and the rule of the Dominion, thus allowing the Aethari Order to be restored.

## Prelude

### Background

Unlike the Battle of Ostara, the last major battle between the League to Restore the Concord and the Dominion of Astra, which had taken place on the ice world of Kryvel, had resulted in a disastrous defeat for the League. The loss of the recently established Frostpoint Base forced the League High Command to break the League Fleet into small task forces that would constantly jump from one star system to another to prevent another defeat for Dominion propagandists to celebrate. Still, the Battle of Kryvel showed the rebellion would escape and survive, instead of stand and fight, if discovered. As such, the key to destroying the rebellion laid not with finding its gathering area but luring it to a choke point. However, the Battle of Ostara showed that, in order to trick the rebels into staying in one place to fight, the Dominion would need a great threat the rebels had no choice but to face.

During Scarlet Veil's ill-fated war against the Dominion, Dawn agents Bevelyn and Jon Melton revealed the existence of the second Void Core to the Free League. The Pelari Spynet, in time, intercepted information that revealed the Dominion was building the station in orbit over the forest moon of Sylvar. The Dominion had occupied the moon for the Void Core project, thereby bringing Dominion forces into conflict with the native Nubbits. With the Dominion largely paying the Nubbits no mind, only seeing them as annoyances instead of a threat, they failed to recognize the dangers the native lifeforms could cause and did not notice the species had a strong warrior culture.

Thanks to the Pelari spies, the League also learned that Galactic Sovereign Aldus Crayle, otherwise known as the Dark Lord of the Morrak Xarn Nocturne, was to oversee the completion of the Void Core II, the construction of which was otherwise being overseen by Prefect Tiaan Velkor. Unbeknownst to the League, the Sovereign had allowed the location of the second Void Core to be leaked and established a trap, foreseeing the League's plan. The Dominion had leaked the location of the new Void Core with the hope the totemic symbolism the battle station held would draw out the rebellion for a major battle, in which the Free League Navy could be crushed in a surprise trap. Beyond that, the Dominion ensured that the rebels learned that Crayle himself would be present, thereby making the Void Core an even more important target for the rebels.

### Baiting the trap

To bait the trap, Dominion Intelligence leaked the information that fell into the hands of the Pelari spies, ensuring the League obtained incomplete information about the Void Core: the League learned that Crayle would be present but was tricked into thinking that the battle station's superlaser was inoperative, when in fact the battle station would be fully operational. In addition to maintaining a skilled legion on Sylvar's surface to counter a rebel ground assault, which the League would need to launch to disable the Void Core shield generator on Sylvar's surface, the Sovereign would also hide a large fleet of Dreadhulls on the far side of Sylvar so as to entrap the fleet. However, Prefect Derrek Raythe had sent a message containing orders for Death Squadron to join the fleet at Sylvar. To keep the information secretive, commander Iden Versio from the special forces Inferno Squad, was sent to the Invincible Faith and deleted it.

Ultimately, the Dominion prepared itself to face the rebellion on a spacefront and battlefront under its complete control, with the looming threat of the Nubbits not something the regime realized would be a danger —unbeknownst to the Dominion personnel, the Nubbits actively prepared to attack the bunker and, over the course of several weeks, established defensive positions around the facility which would prove vital in the battle to come. So as to capture the inevitable rebel ground team, Crayle selected Tempest Flux, an elite formation composed of some of his finest troops, to be garrisoned on Sylvar, where Tempest Flux reinforced the shield generator personnel. As the deployment continued, whole squads were lost to the dark forests of Sylvar.
