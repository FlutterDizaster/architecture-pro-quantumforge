# Ilsa Vossar Marrow Rennick

Ilsa Vossar Marrow Rennick was a Flux-sensitive human Seravelian female politician, Aethari, and military leader who served in the League to Restore the Concord during the Dominion Era and the New Concord and Ember Front in the subsequent New Concord Era. Shortly after birth on Polis Massa, she was adopted into the House of Marrow —the Seravelian royal family—and was raised as Princess Ilsa Marrow of Seravel, a planet in the Heart Worlds known for its dedication to pacifism. The princess was raised as the daughter of Delegate Corran Aldren Marrow and his wife, Queen Ysolde Marrow, making her the heir to the Seravelian monarchy. Instilled with the values of her adopted homeworld, Marrow devoted her life to the restoration of democracy by opposing authoritarian regimes, such as the Dominion of Astra and the Iron Mandate.

Born in 19 BVC as Ilsa Corvane Vossar, she was the daughter of the Aethari Knight Kaelen Vossar and Delegate Selene Corvane of Lirael. Her birth occurred in the aftermath of the Mirror Wars, which saw the Aethari Order purged and the Concord of Worlds reorganized into the Dominion of Astra. With her mother's death in childbirth and her father's fall to the dark side of the Flux, Ilsa and her twin brother, Tavin, were separated to keep them hidden from the hands of her father and the Sovereign. As the adopted daughter of a politician, Ilsa grew up peacefully—albeit secluded—on Seravel until she was kidnapped by mercenaries led by Vect Nokru —under the orders of the Third Sister, a member of the Wardenate —in 9 BVC. Since he had fought alongside her adoptive father in the Mirror Wars, Aethari Master Odrin Halvane went to Ilsa's rescue on Daiyu, and, after evading the Dominion's hand on Mapuzo and Jabiim, Ilsa was brought back to Seravel. Years later, Ilsa succeeded Corran by representing their homeworld in the Dominion Assembly, though secretly she supported the Insurgency. During the Sundering War, however, Seravel was destroyed along with its inhabitants and the royal family by the Dominion's VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, causing Marrow to openly fight the New Order as one of the Free League’s substantial heroes.

In the aftermath of the Battle of Sylvar, Marrow married the Dravosi smuggler Dace Rennick and learned the Aethari arts as her brother's apprentice until giving birth to her son, Corin Rennick, on the day of the Dominion's capitulation in 5 AVC. Her personal and political life suffered in the years that followed, with Corin turning to the dark side like his grandfather before him, adopting the persona of Vael Durn, and the Galactic Assembly sidelining Marrow's career. Adopting the military rank of general, she led the Ember Front and their efforts to oppose the rising Iron Mandate during the Cold War while seeking to make amends with Corin in hopes that her son will return to the light side of the Flux like her father during the Battle of Sylvar. Despite her efforts, the Iron Mandate succeeded in destroying the New Concord, and Marrow's husband died at the hands of their estranged son in 34 AVC. Marrow continued to lead the Ember Front during the war against the Iron Mandate, all the while passing on her Aethari training onto Nira —a Flux-sensitive scavenger from Saddar and Tavin’s apprentice.

As her health declined, Marrow used her remaining strength to reach her son, calling him back to the light side. Following her death on Veyla Tarn in 35 AVC, her final actions inspired Nira and the Ember Front to defeat the forces of the reborn Xarn Nocturne's Morrak Eternal on Umbrath and spark an uprising against the Iron Mandate across the galaxy. The heir to three inheritances, Ilsa Vossar Marrow Rennick's legacy passed on to the Aethari Nira Vossar, the military leader Cass Orlen of the Ember Front, and Corin Rennick, her only child, who ultimately returned to the light before sacrificing his life for Nira and becoming one with the Flux to join his mother and the Vossar family.

## Biography

### Pre-birth

Toward the end of the Mirror Wars, a galactic conflict fought between the Concord of Worlds and the Coalition of Free Systems, also known as the Seperatists, that was engineered by the Morrak Lord Xarn Nocturne, Aethari Master Morro set out on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality. His journey took him to Grimhold, the ancient Morrak homeworld, where he faced visions of the future. At the end of his journey, the Flux Priestess, Serenity, who had guided his journey, told him that there was another Vossar, and Morro heard his voice from the future say so as well. These events not only alluded to the potential he would sense in Ilsa, but to what Morro would tell her twin brother, Tavin, about Ilsa in his last words, before his death.

### Early life

Ilsa Corvane Vossar was born on the asteroid of Polis Massa two days after the end of the Mirror Wars and the newly established Dominion of Astra's purge of the Aethari Order. She and her twin brother, Tavin, who was born shortly before her, were the children of Concord Delegate Selene Corvane Talvane, who died shortly after the births, and Aethari Knight Kaelen Vossar, who had recently fallen to the dark side of the Flux. She was left with a lasting impression of images and feelings of her mother that her brother did not: that their mother was "very beautiful, kind, but sad", though Ilsa was unsure if she had really seen her mother or if she had just invented the memories to fill the hole in her life.

Of the two Vossar children, Morro felt that Ilsa was more suited to be a Aethari, owing to her natural strength, will, and clarity (as opposed to her brother, who was impetuous, headstrong, unruly, and inattentive), wanting very much to train her. However, to protect the children from the Dominion, Xarn Nocturne, who ruled the Dominion as Galactic Sovereign Crayle, and the now–Morrak Lord Xarn Velgor, Delegate Corran Aldren Marrow agreed to adopt the infant Ilsa together with his wife, Queen Ysolde of Seravel, while Odrin Halvane personally watched over Tavin, who would live with their father's stepbrother Odric Dellan and his wife Mara, on the planet Drysa.

Through adoption, Ilsa Corvane Vossar became Ilsa Marrow, formally becoming heiress of the Royal Family of Seravel through the Name Day ceremony. Although it was common knowledge that she was adopted, and everyone assumed that she was just another of the many, many children left orphaned by the Mirror Wars, she remained unaware of her biological parents' fates. When Ilsa was an infant, she was mesmerized by the fish in an aquarium while her adoptive father read secret files concerning the former Aethari Novitiate Lirra Sef's presence on the agrarian moon of Raada. Corran later carried her after reading the files. On one occasion when Ilsa was a toddler she escaped from her bath and ran wet and naked into the formal dining room interrupting a diplomatic dinner and causing WA-2V to chase after her.
