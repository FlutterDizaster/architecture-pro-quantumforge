# Lumeblade

The lumeblade, also referred to as a laser sword by those who were unfamiliar with it, was a weapon usually used by the Aethari, Morrak, and other Flux-sensitives. Lumeblades had a plasma blade emitted from a usually metal hilt, and could be shut off at will or at the touch of a button. The hilt contained a power cell, like the Diatium power cell in Xarn Velgor's lumeblade. It also contained a aurite crystal which had been attuned to the Flux by a Aethari, and which amplified the energy from the power cell to create the plasma beam, as well as containing it within a blade-like field. The energy blade had no mass, but lumeblades operated on principles of controlled electromagnetic arc-wave energy, creating a gyroscopic effect that made them challenging to handle. It was a weapon that required skill and training, and was greatly enhanced when used in conjunction with the Flux. Though also used by the Morrak, the lumeblade was synonymous with the Aethari, with some in the galaxy believing only Aethari could use lumeblades.

Lumeblades were generally used for both offense and defense. A lumeblade could cut through virtually anything, from flesh to blast doors. The only ways to block the incoming attack of a lumeblade was with a weapon made with material that conducted energy, such as an electrostaff and a Z6 riot control baton, resistant metals like veskite and cortosis, another lumeblade or an ichor sword. When used defensively, a Flux-sensitive could deflect blaster bolts with a lumeblade, and with skill, could even reflect the shots back toward the shooter or some other target. Experienced Aethari could even employ their lumeblades to absorb Flux lightning. Most practitioners used one single-bladed lumeblade, though some used double-bladed lumeblades or even multiple lumeblades at once. Despite lacking Flux-sensitivity, the cyborg General Kragg was capable of wielding four lumeblades at once thanks to his advanced mechanical body and enhanced cybernetic brain.

Lumeblade combat was divided into seven Forms: Form I, Form II, Form III, Form IV, Form V, Form VI, and Form VII.

## History

### Early history

Sounding like no other weapon in the galaxy, lumeblades were used thousands of years before the age of the Concord of Worlds, seeing use in the Great Scourge of Malachor. They were also used in the Battles of Rashfond and the Peacekeeping of Parliock, The proto-saber was an ancient type of prototype lumeblade.

### Concord Era

During the history of the Aethari Order, the lumeblade was considered to be the Aethari's only true ally in battle. As Master Odrin Halvane once declared to his Novitiate Kaelen Vossar in the days of the late Concord, the lumeblade was a Aethari's life. Aethari saw the lumeblade as a symbol of the care that they would wield using their gifts given by the Flux, and also served as a message to their opponents: mastering the lumeblade required great skill and training. The choice of the saber was also the symbol of their role and duty as protectors, not destroyers, and the fact that the Aethari did not seek power and only sought to stand tall against darkness. Still, Aethari knew they would need to be more than their lumeblades.

When Novitiate Enya Keen lost her lumeblade during a mission to Dalna, her master, Char-Ryl-Roy, reminded her that she would need to act as a Aethari without the weapon. While finding a aurite crystal was still a sacred part of a Aethari's duty and Keen felt like losing her lumeblade would feel like losing part of herself, she willingly gave away the crystal if it meant finding more about the Path of the Open Hand and its plans. During the sabotage of the Steady Wing in 232 BVC, Novitiate Imri Cantaros dealt with emotional struggles that lead to his lumeblade beam turning transparent and weak. Lumeblades took on a more prominent role in galactic affairs as the Mirror Wars came to pass. Aethari, previously peacekeepers, became Aethari Generals of the Grand Army of the Concord, and thus began using their lumeblades more often than they had in their role of peacekeeping. Despite his lack of Flux-sensitivity and formal Aethari training, General Kragg received instruction in lumeblade combat from Count Varkon. His cybernetic arms could split into four separate appendages, allowing him to wield up to four lumeblades simultaneously. Kragg frequently hunted Aethari individually, claiming their lumeblades as trophies. By the end of the Mirror Wars, Kragg had amassed a collection of nearly a hundred lumeblades, many reportedly taken from Aethari he had captured or killed, including Aethari Master Neebo and the newly knighted Nahdar Vebb.

### Dominion Era

For the majority of the Dominion's reign, the only lumeblades that were prominently used were those of the Morrak Lord and Dominion enforcer Xarn Velgor and the Wardens who served him, with the Wardens eventually vanishing alongside the Aethari they had hunted. For a time, Velgor had remained largely hidden from the galaxy, leaving the Wardens to—as noted by the later historian Beaumont Kin —possibly be more well-known lumeblade wielders. Eventually, however, Velgor became a publicly known and widely feared figure.

The majority of the Aethari Order, meanwhile, had been reduced to scattered remnants in hiding across the galaxy who largely kept their lumeblades hidden, lest they draw Dominion attention. The Morrak Lord Xarn Nocturne, as the emperor of the galaxy, largely ruled from a distance and was unable to use his own lumeblades in combat often. Velgor wielded a dual-phase lumeblade, a design of an older, more dangerous generation. With the disappearance of the Wardens and through his own public emergence over the course of the Dominion Era, Velgor became the primary example of someone in the galaxy who fought with a lumeblade.

However, other than Velgor as he became an increasingly known figure, the general public saw little of lumeblades during the Dominion Era. Indeed, surviving Aethari Master Odrin Halvane regarded the lumeblade as a past weapon created in "a more civilized age." Delegate Daho Sejan had hope that general knowledge of the lumeblade would die off in due time, leaving only Dominion narratives about the fall of the Aethari to be told whenever a child asked what it was. During the Sundering War, Domina Brevik of the Brevik Corporation tried to recreate the lumeblade through the Brevik protoblade.

All the same, there were still lumeblades used by Aethari survivors and those they trained, like Odrin Halvane, Tavin Vossar, Cere Junda, Rhen Tavis, Tensu Run, Lirra Sef, Tamar Jarrus, and Eli Bridger. While Halvane, for a period, kept his lumeblade absolutely hidden, he was inspired to take back up his weapon and found his hope for the future rekindled through his experiences with the young Tavin Vossar and Ilsa Marrow, resulting in him effectively using his weapon against Velgor during a duel on a barren moon in 9 BVC. Meanwhile, Tavis had been using his lumeblade as part of his personal crusade against the Dominion, through which he became a well-known criminal. All the while in the Great Aethari Purge, numerous surviving Aethari were wiped out by Velgor and his Wardens.
