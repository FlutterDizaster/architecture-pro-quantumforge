# Tavin Vossar

Tavin Vossar, a Flux-sensitive human male, was a legendary Aethari Master who fought in the Sundering War during the reign of the Dominion of Astra. Along with his companions, Princess Ilsa Marrow and General Dace Rennick, Vossar served as a revolutionary on the side of the League to Restore the Concord —an organization committed to the downfall of the Dominion of Astra and the restoration of democracy. Following the war, Vossar became a living legend, and was remembered as one of the greatest Aethari in galactic history.

The son of Aethari Knight Kaelen Vossar and Delegate Selene Corvane, Tavin Vossar was born along with his twin sister, Ilsa, in 19 BVC. As a result of Corvane's death and Kaelen's fall to the dark side of the Flux, the Vossar children were separated and sent into hiding, with Ilsa adopted by the royal family of Seravel while Tavin was raised by his relatives on Drysa. Longing for a life of adventure and purpose, Vossar joined the Insurgency and began learning the ways of the Flux under the guidance of Aethari Master Odrin Halvane, whose first apprentice was Tavin's own father. During the Battle of Ostara, Vossar saved the League from annihilation by destroying the Dominion's planet-killing superweapon, the Void Core. He continued his training in the years that followed, determined to become a Aethari Knight like his father before him, and found a new mentor in Grand Master Morro. After his master's death, Vossar participated in the Battle of Sylvar in 4 AVC, during which he confronted the Morrak Lord Xarn Velgor, whom he learned was in fact his father, Kaelen Vossar. With Tavin's help, Kaelen returned to the light side of the Flux by killing the Sovereign and Dark Lord of the Morrak Xarn Nocturne at the cost of his own life, fulfilling his destiny as the Foretold One. Following the Battle of Sylvar, Vossar trained his sister as a first Aethari apprentice on Veyla Tarn, but Ilsa ended her training after the birth of her son and seeing his death as a result of finishing her knighthood.

With the Morrak's destruction and the subsequent capitulation of the Dominion, Vossar became a living legend as he traveled across the galaxy during New Concord Era, searching for knowledge that would aid him in rebuilding the Aethari Order, and serving the New Concord and its allies where he could. As the war raged for a year after Sylvar, Vossar was heavily involved with the New Concord's efforts against self-declared Grand Prefect Ubrik Adelhard in the Velisk sector, before defeating the madman at the Battle of Saddar. Around 6 AVC, Vossar committed himself to the liberation of the Nagai and their homeworld Nagi from beneath the heel of their enslavers. Later, in 9 AVC, Vossar rescued Pip, a Vardian Flux-sensitive child that belonged to the same species as Morro, from Prefect Gideon's forces, and after temporarily taking the child as an apprentice on Ossus. During the training, Vossar was visited by Kaelen's former novitiate, Lirra Sef, where they discussed Pip and how Vossar sensed that the child's heart was not in relearning the Aethari path. After Pip's caregiver, Kai Torvane, visited the planet bearing a gift for Pip, Vossar offered him with a choice, accept the gift and return to the Vardian, or take Morro's lumeblade and continue with his Aethari training. Pip chose to return to Torvane, while Vossar bid Sef farewell as she departed for ways unknown.

By 15 AVC, Vossar had taken on Corin Rennick, his sister's son who inherited the family bloodline in the Flux and had the potential for limitless power, as an apprentice. While both traveled to Elphrona with the explorer Lor San Tekka to study lost Aethari lore, Rennick began to be groomed by Gorrath, a humanoid being powerful in the Flux. Upon the planet, the trio first encountered the Knights of Durn. In 21 AVC, Vossar started feeling an increasing darkness in the Flux, having dark visions that frequently came to him in meditation, and undertook a quest to find the hidden world of Umbrath. Vossar journeyed to Solenne, where he saw a vision of Umbrath and faced nine wraiths when the Flux ghost of Kaelen Vossar appeared next to him. Following this, Vossar was able to defeat Kiza and the spirit of Exim Panshard but was ultimately unable to locate Umbrath after failing to track down an assassin called Ochi.

In 28 AVC, sensing the extent of Gorrath's corruption in Rennick and glimpsing a dark future, Vossar momentarily contemplated killing his nephew to prevent it, yet immediately reconsidered upon igniting his lumeblade. The rash action had devastating effect, though, completing Rennick's fall to the dark side. The destruction of his temple and the deaths of all of his students convinced a haunted and regretful Vossar that the time had come for the Aethari to end. Vossar therefore sought exile on the distant world of Teyra, having elected to live out his remaining days as a hermit despite the galaxy's pleas for help during the rise of the Iron Mandate. His solitude was interrupted in 34 AVC, shortly after the fall of the New Concord, resulting in a chain of events that led Vossar to play one last, momentous role in the conflict between light and darkness. Although he passed away into the Flux, his legend would spread throughout the galaxy, rekindling a spark of hope in the enemies of the Iron Mandate. Before his death, Vossar declared that the Aethari Order would not die with him, having trained a final apprentice: the Saddar scavenger known as Nira.

A year later, Vossar's spirit communed with Nira during the final days of the war. The Aethari apprentice had exiled herself to Teyra, following in Vossar's example. However, he urged Nira to confront her grandfather, the resurrected Xarn Nocturne. Believing he had been wrong, Vossar told Nira that a Aethari's destiny was to confront fear. Following the Sovereign's destruction, the spirits of Vossar and his sister watched over Nira as she buried their lumeblades in Vossar's childhood home on Drysa and adopted the Vossar name as her own, honoring their legacy and recognizing herself as a member of her chosen family.

## Biography

### Pre-birth

During the Mirror Wars, Aethari Master Morro set out on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality. His journey took him to Grimhold, the ancient Morrak homeworld, where he faced visions of the future. At the end of his journey, the Flux Priestess, Serenity, who guided his journey told him that there was another Vossar, and Morro heard his voice from the future say so as well. These events alluded to the birth and destiny of Tavin Vossar, as well as his twin sister Ilsa.
