# Jask Morvain

Jask Morvain was a human male crime lord and former bounty hunter whose career spanned decades, from the fall of the Concord of Worlds to the end of the rule of the Dominion of Astra. Originally code-named Alpha, he was an unaltered replicant of the famed Vardian bounty hunter, Dren Morvain, who raised the boy as his son. Jask emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of Vardian armor. His personal starship was the Slave I, a Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft that once belonged to Dren. Trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, Morvain was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy during the reign of Sovereign Crayle. He became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the Dominion and the extensive criminal underworld. After living among the Dunewalker Raiders of Drysa for a time, he took over as Daimyo of Dune Reach during the era of the New Concord.

Jask was created on the Wild Space planet of Thalassa as a result of an arrangement between the Morrak Lord Xarn Vexus and Dren Morvain, a Vardian foundling who served as the template of the Grand Army of the Concord. Unlike the replicant troopers grown from Dren's DNA, Dren regarded Jask as his son. In accordance with certain Vardian traditions, Jask was also considered a foundling by virtue of his descent from Dren, whose chain code documented his participation in the Vardian Civil War and his ownership of the armor later inherited by Jask. During the last days of the Secessionist Crisis, Jask and Dren retreated to Karrik where the latter was killed in battle by Aethari Master Torvin Kell. Orphaned by Dren's death, Jask sought vengeance by attempting to assassinate Kell, although his plan proved unsuccessful. After serving time in prison on Valcora, Morvain took up his late father's profession as a bounty hunter, working alongside other mercenaries, such as Skreel, Volk, and Asajj Varess, while also notably apprenticing himself to his father's infamous brief protégée, Cad Kesh.

In the aftermath of the Mirror Wars, Morvain continued to make a name for himself while working for the gangster Hrask Garrak Tiure, as well as the Morrak Lord Xarn Velgor. During the Sundering War, the bounty hunter managed to track down Captain Dace Rennick to Nimbara on a contract for the Dominion of Astra, hoping to deliver him to Hrask's Palace on Drysa. During the journey, Morvain stopped on the moon of Nar Shaddaa in order to make sure Rennick would survive his time in stasium, but he lost the smuggler while he was away as part of the return of the Scarlet Veil, kicking off the War of the Bounty Hunters. Ultimately, Morvain regained Rennick and delivered him to the impatient Hrask.

Morvain tried to prevent Rennick's rescue by the League to Restore the Concord, only to fall into the Great Pit of Carkoon when the smuggler accidentally slammed a pole into the bounty hunter's jetpack, causing it to malfunction. Although Morvain survived his ordeal with the man-eating maw-pit, his armor was sold to Cobb Vanth by Skerrit scavengers. Following a time in the company of a Dunewalker tribe, Morvain eventually regained his armor by tracking down and confronting the Vardian warrior Kai Torvane on Solenne, who had taken Morvain's armor from Vanth. After helping Torvane to rescue the foundling Pip from the clutches of Prefect Gideon, the ex-bounty-hunter returned to Drysa where he killed Fen Ortuna and claimed the late Hrask's throne as Daimyo, with Fennec Shand by his side. Now the head of his own gotra, Morvain intended to rule the territories of Dune Reach by winning the people's respect, though he would face several major challenges to his position owed to the two-facedness of mayor Mok Shaiz such as the Twins ' bid for power and the expansion of the Krell Syndicate's spice trade. Ultimately, though, Morvain overcame all obstacles - including a confrontation with his old mentor, Cad Kesh - and emerged victorious with the help of his associates, gaining the appreciation and approval of Dune Reach's denizens.

## Biography

### Early life

Around 33 BVC, an extensive replicant army was secretly commissioned on Thalassa by Aethari Master Talo-Venn, who believed it would be necessary in order to ensure the protection of the Concord of Worlds. The army, however, was later taken over by the Morrak in 32 BVC shortly after the Battle of Lirael and was used as a plot to overthrow the Concord and destroy the Aethari Order. Prior to the creation process, Vardian bounty hunter Dren Morvain was recruited by the Morrak Lord Xarn Vexus. Dren was to be the genetic template for the replicant army. Dren agreed to become the genetic template for a considerable payment, as well as one request: the creation of a replicant who did not have the same genetic modifications, such as behavioral conditioning and growth acceleration. The Thalassan scientists agreed and created a boy codenamed "Alpha."

Although he was born a replicant, Alpha regarded Dren as his father, who likewise viewed the boy as his son. Soon after referred to as Jask, he was the very first of many replicants modeled on the Dren Morvain template, but was only one of two known replicants that were pure genetic copies of the original Morvain template, the other being Omega.

For the next ten years, Jask was raised on Thalassa by his father, primarily within Tipoca City and occasionally accompanied Dren on missions to track down countless bounties. On one night, Jask awoke to see the Slave I flying off, watching his father leave on one of his missions. Despite the isolation of the cities on Thalassa he was raised in, the young Morvain enjoyed the combat training his father administered to him and danger-filled life provided by the man who raised him. Dren taught his replicated son many of the skills he had learned as a bounty hunter, so Jask learned survival and martial skills and became proficient with a blaster as a child. He also underwent replicant trooper training and became familiar with members of the Thalassan government. From a young age, Jask also learned to pilot his father's ship, the Slave I, including learning how to command and operate its weapon systems.

For the boy's first mission in 23 BVC, Jask and Dren Morvain alongside Neelda, Rinn, and Tiver were hired by the father of a female Twi'lek to capture his daughter and bring her back alive. The bounty hunters were surprised that a young boy would be joining them, but they knew not to anger Jask's father and agreed to it. While on the way to the job, Jask asked Dren why they even needed a crew for the job and wished they went with a more famous bounty hunter like Cad Kesh. Dren replied that the crew was the unknown factor which was Jask's lesson for the job.
