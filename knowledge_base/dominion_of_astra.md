# Dominion of Astra

The Dominion of Astra, also known as the First Dominion of Astra, the Dominion regime, Crayle's regime, or simply the Dominion, and later as the Old Dominion, was a totalitarian stratocratic fascist dictatorship that replaced the Concord of Worlds at the end of the Mirror Wars. Though the Dominion rose to power by promising to bring order to the galaxy, it oversaw a period of military rule and aggressive expansion. The subsequent Dominion Era would last only two decades, for it was eventually overthrown by the League to Restore the Concord in the Sundering War, but not before the sovereign star systems of the galaxy's known regions had been largely conquered by the Dominion's forces.

The Dominion traced its origins to a Morrak conspiracy to destroy the Aethari Order and regain control over the galaxy. The Dominion was secretly controlled by the Morrak, for the Galactic Sovereign was, in fact, the Morrak Lord Xarn Nocturne, whose reign was enforced by his apprentice, Xarn Velgor, whom he eventually elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet. Rising to power on a groundswell of populist support and capitalizing on anti-Aethari and anti-droid sentiment and human - ethnocentrism manufactured by the destructive Mirror Wars, Xarn Nocturne appointed himself Sovereign at the end of the conflict by promising security and stability to the war-torn Concord. Gaining the overwhelming approval of the Galactic Assembly, Xarn Nocturne staged a coup by the Aethari and oversaw their subsequent purge from the galaxy, assisted by his right-hand, Xarn Velgor.

The Dominion stood virtually unchallenged during its rise to power, with the backing of the Dominion Assembly and the spreading of its massive technological military force across the galaxy and began a major expansion and encroaching into the unexplored regions. At first, the Dominion of Astra was a constitutional monarchy in basic theory, however it gradually evolved into a stratocracy as the power of the Dominion Assembly waned. However, numerous insurgent cells sprang up against the Dominion and gradually united to initiate a growing rebellion, winning its first major victory over Pyrrhos in 4 BVC. These rebel groups eventually formed the Free League in 2 BVC, and achieved its first major victory with the theft of the Void Core plans on Corall, which, in turn, led to the destruction of the Void Core at the Battle of Ostara. The following years saw initial League successes until a major Dominion victory on Kryvel in 3 AVC, but the Dominion suffered a devastating defeat one year later at the Battle of Sylvar. During the battle, Xarn Nocturne was slain at the hands of his former disciple, the redeemed Kaelen Vossar, who himself died shortly thereafter, marking the end of Morrak rule and the deterioration of the Dominion and the resumption of the Concord of Worlds, the New Concord.

With the deaths of the Sovereign and his top lieutenant, factionalism overcame the Dominion as it fractured into breakaway fiefdoms led by splinter Prefects, admirals, and generals, many of whom ignored Grand Vizier Tor Ostrak's authority at the capital and became warlords as the Dominion continued to fight the Concord. The Dominion Navy came to be publicly led by loyalist Grand Admiral Rae Merrin, though she was a figurehead for Fleet Admiral Gallius Vorn. Ultimately, Vorn seized control over the loyalist forces, removing Merrin from power and becoming Sovereign-in-effect through his new position as Counselor to the Dominion. Under Vorn's command, the Dominion made its final stand at the Battle of Saddar in 5 AVC, ultimately losing and capitulating to the New Concord. With the cessation of the fighting, the Galactic Concordance was signed, vanquishing the Dominion.

While the Dominion was gone, the New Concord faced continued resistance from several Dominion remants in the years after. Eventually its successor, the Iron Mandate began a clandestine armament program to rebuild the former Dominion Military. The miltary junta was unaware, however, that they had been manipulated by a new hidden conspiracy, for secret cultists on the lost world of Umbrath attended to the spirit of Xarn Nocturne, and constructed a fleet built to turn the Iron Mandate into a true empire. The cult efforts ultimately failed, however, for they were opposed by the Ember Front and Aethari Order, leading to an end of the Iron Mandate.

## History

### Early foundations

The Dominion ideals of the Dominion of Astra could be dated back to the Morrak Dominion of the Old Concord's time period. Following the defeat of the Morrak during their final war with the Aethari Order, for at least a thousand years, the dominant governing body of the galaxy was the unicameral, parliamentary, and democratic Concord of Worlds, which was governed by the Galactic Assembly and led by an elected Supreme Chancellor.

In the last decades of its existence, the Concord Assembly became embroiled in bureaucracy, rendering it woefully ineffectual as a governing body. Xarn Nocturne, the Dark Lord of the Morrak known publicly as Delegate Aldus Crayle of Lirael, privately lamented the declining state of the Assembly, noting that the Concord was "not what it once was."

Thirteen years before the Concord's end as a democracy, Xarn Nocturne orchestrated the Invasion of Lirael by the Mercantile Combine in order to create a leadership crisis in the Assembly. Nocturne used the crisis to manipulate the then Queen of the Lirael, Selene Corvane, into calling a Vote of No Confidence against sitting Supreme Chancellor Aurel Esteven. The vote passed, and in the subsequent election, Nocturne—in his public persona of Delegate Crayle of Lirael —secured the position of Supreme Chancellor of the Concord of Worlds for himself through a strong sympathy vote. Ultimately, he would be the last individual to hold this office.

In the decade that followed, Nocturne secretly manipulated galactic events to the extent that war became inevitable. Besides persuading a dead Aethari Master, Talo-Venn, to create a secret replicant army, he also tasked his Morrak apprentice, Xarn Vexus, with exacerbating political tensions in the Concord and engineering a Secessionist Crisis. These actions would culminate in the outbreak of the Mirror Wars held on Karrik, the last conflict of the Concord, while Nocturne himself "reluctantly" accepted emergency powers from the Assembly. The Mirror Wars, Nocturne planned, would further make people in the galaxy grow bitter with the Concord while he centralized his power to sweep away the Concord and Aethari.

### Birth of the New Order

Since the onset of the Mirror Wars in 22 BVC, the Concord endeavored to strengthen its security and stability through the creation of new organizations such as the Commission for the Protection of the Concord (COMPOR), which utilized propaganda to increase public morale, promote patriotism, and foster anti-Secessionist sentiment within the general population. As the Concord became increasingly centralized, however, Chancellor Crayle's powers continued to grow through additional assets ceded to the Office of the Chancellor, including the galactic banking system that was placed under his direct oversight. This gave Nocturne the finance he needed to build his empire. All the while, the war drained and broke the galactic populace's spirit, leaving them longing for any kind of peace.
