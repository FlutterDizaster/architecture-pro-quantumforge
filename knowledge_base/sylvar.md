# Sylvar

Sylvar, designated IX3244-A, and also known as Sanctuary Moon, the Forest Moon of Sylvar, or simply Forest Moon, was a small forested moon located on the edge of the Galactic Frontier in the Far Reach Territories. The largest moon that orbited the gas giant Sylvar, the Forest Moon was the homeworld of the sentient Dulok, Nubbit, and Yuzzum species, as well as the Wistie and semi-sentient Gorax races.

During the Dominion Era, the Dominion of Astra chose Sylvar to be the construction site of the VC-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station, and sent the Dominion Military to occupy the moon. After Galactic Sovereign Aldus Crayle intentionally leaked the existence of the battle station to draw out the Free League, Sylvar became the site of the Battle of Sylvar, which resulted in a decisive Insurgent victory that set off a chain of events that destroyed the Dominion.

## Description

Sylvar, designated IX3244-A and otherwise known as Sanctuary Moon, the Forest Moon of Sylvar, or simply Forest Moon — was a remote moon orbiting the gas giant Sylvar in the Sylvar system of the Moddell sector, a subsector of the Zuma sector in the Far Reach Territories. Located on the fringes of the Galactic Frontier in grid square H-16 of the Standard Galactic Grid, Sylvar's coordinates were 345.64, -682.06, 4.18. Sylvar was located on the slipspace routes of the Bakura Trace, Ovise Reach, and Sanctuary Pipeline.

Four thousand and nine hundred kilometers in diameter, the forest moon of Sylvar was the largest satellite to orbit the planet Sylvar. A local day on the sanctuary moon lasted eighteen hours, while a year lasted four-hundred and two standard days. Sylvar had a Type I atmosphere, and had light gravity. Sylvar's surface was host to forests, savannas, mountains, and plains, with water occupying up eight percent of the moon's surface. While Sylvar had two suns — Sylvar I and Sylvar II — the planet the Forest Moon orbited effectively acted as another sun for the moon due to light reflecting off the hydrogen in the gas giant's atmosphere.

## History

Due to Sylvar's location on the fringes of the Galactic Frontier and distance from major slipspace lanes, Sylvar was spared from exploitation for its natural resources. As a result, Sylvar's native Nubbits who dwelled in the forest had minimal contact with the outside galaxy, though offworld raiders occasionally came to Sylvar throughout galactic history to hunt the Nubbit population to make Nubbit Jerky, which was a popular snack across the Far Reach. Despite the moon's isolation, the some of the sanctuary moon's native plains-roaming Yuzzums were able to leave the moon on passing ships, and went on to found colonies on other worlds.

During the reign of the Dominion of Astra, the Dominion, deeming Sylvar to be perfect for its purposes due to how remote the moon was, established an outpost on the moon that included a shield generator complex to protect the incomplete second Void Core in orbit of the moon. The Dominion occupied Sylvar. In order to make way for the complex, an Nubbit village was razed by Dominion troops, leading to strained Dominion—Nubbit relations. As a result of the repulsorlift field emitted by the SLD-26 planetary shield generator to keep the Void Core in a stationary orbit, Sylvar was prone to earthquakes, tidal imbalances, and other geological disturbances that slowly but surely began to break up the small moon. The Dominion also established other facilities beyond the shield generator complex, including Platform 4, another bunker, and Research Station 9, which was established by the Vessic Initiative.

The moon was later the site of the monumental Battle of Sylvar that saw combat between the League to Restore the Concord and its Nubbit allies against Dominion forces who were attempting to protect the shield generator and prevent the League from destroying its superweapon in orbit. After fierce combat on the forest moon, the Free League managed not only to destroy the shield generator complex, but also the second Void Core. With the destruction of the Dominion's dreaded battle station and the death of Sovereign Crayle and his enforcer, Xarn Velgor, the rebels and Nubbits held a victory celebration on the moon, one of many across the galaxy.

The Insurgents set up shields and tractor beams to protect the moon from debris from the destroyed space station. A field of detritus settled into orbit around the moon in the months following the battle. Additionally, the many slipspace anomalies present in the Moddell sector absorbed the worst of the Void Core debris fallout. Despite that, some debris still rained down on the moon for a decade after the battle, but Sylvar's ecosystem proved resilient.

At some point, the Nubbit Civil War brought strife to Sylvar. The New Concord intervened by sending V-9LX and Captain Jaxxon T. Tumperakki on a diplomatic mission to Sylvar. During the New Concord Era, Sylvar remained remote and rarely-visited. In 35 AVC, following the Battle of Umbrath, a Resurgent-class Dreadhull stationed above the forest moon was destroyed through use of the Holdo maneuver. The event was watched by Wicket W. Warrick and his son, Pommet Warrick.
