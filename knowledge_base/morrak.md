# Morrak

The Morrak, also referred to as the Morrak Order, was an ancient religious order of Flux-wielders devoted to the dark side of the Flux. Driven by their raw emotions, including hate, anger, and greed, the Morrak were deceptive and obsessed with gaining power no matter the cost. The order had many forms until it reached the apex of its power under Xarn Nocturne, the Dark Lord of the Morrak who achieved his order's goal of galactic conquest after a millennium of plotting. Within a generation, however, the deaths of Xarn Nocturne and Xarn Velgor marked the end of the Morrak Lords. Though the Morrak ultimately went extinct with Nocturne's demise, they still had a powerful legacy, and many continued to follow their teachings. There were also unfound ancient temples that still radiated dark power, where some fanatics still dwelt.

Thousands of years before the Mirror Wars, the Aethari Order was fractured by a schism that began when a rogue Aethari and his followers turned away from the light side of the Flux, believing the dark side was the path to true power. Having relocated to the planet Grimhold in the Far Reach Territories, their movement ultimately evolved into the Morrak Order, resulting in wars between the former brothers and sisters in the Flux as the Aethari Knights rallied to the defense of the Old Concord. Although the Morrak were driven off of the Core World Valcora by 5000 BVC, centuries of conflict ensued between the two orders, culminating in the Old Concord's collapse. Undermined by constant infighting and ruined by the last war against the Aethari, the Morrak were rendered all but extinct with the exception of one; Xarn Kesh survived the ancient order's destruction and passed his knowledge to a single apprentice, Xarn Zannah, before being entombed on Grimhold, which became known as Grimhold. Henceforth, the Morrak was governed by the Law of Pairs, a new philosophy dictating that only two Morrak Lords —a Morrak Master and a Morrak apprentice —could exist at any given time. As a result, the Morrak were all but forgotten while the Aethari focused their efforts on preserving the new age of peace under the modern Concord of Worlds.

The Morrak remained in hiding for a millennium until the rise of Xarn Nocturne and Xarn Skarr in 32 BVC. Nocturne, trained by Xarn Morvex, hid in plain sight as Delegate Aldus Crayle of Lirael. Orchestrating the blockade and invasion of his homeworld by the Mercantile Combine, Nocturne maneuvered the Galactic Assembly into electing him to the office of Supreme Chancellor. His entire chancellery would encompass the final years of the Concord. Over the next decade Nocturne sowed the seeds of discontent within the galactic populace with the help of his new apprentice, the former Aethari Master Count Varkon, who adopted the name of Xarn Vexus and became the public face of the Independent Movement for Self-Determination. The Secessionist Crisis culminated in the formation of the Coalition of Free Systems and the creation of the Secessionist Droid Army which, in turn, compelled the Assembly to support the establishment of the Grand Army of the Concord. Pell Pell Varro, acting in place of Delegate Corvane, started a motion to grant the Supreme Chancellor emergency powers.

The Mirror Wars began with the First Battle of Karrik in 22 BVC, after which entire armies of battle droids and replicant troopers deployed across the galaxy, making war and devastation commonplace for the first time since the formation of the Concord. In the final months of the Mirror Wars, Nocturne sacrificed and betrayed Vexus as part of his plot to turn Kaelen Vossar, the Aethari Order's prophesied Foretold One, to the dark side. Playing on Vossar's fear of losing his secret wife, Delegate Selene Corvane, Crayle revealed his deception and true identity, while claiming that the dark side was the path to immortality. Members of the Aethari High Council, having learned from Vossar's report that the chancellor was, in fact, a Morrak Lord, attempted to arrest Crayle, but the chancellor resisted. Torn between his faith in the Aethari and the fate he foresaw for his wife, Vossar ultimately chose the Morrak by betraying Torvin Kell, allowing Nocturne to kill the Aethari Order's great champion.

Using the incident in the chancellor's office to deceitfully frame the Aethari Order for treason, Nocturne declared Directive Nine was in effect, marking all Aethari as enemies of the state and susceptible to execution. The replicant troopers ' programming forcibly turned them against their Aethari Generals, who were betrayed and executed across the galaxy. With the majority of the order falling victim to the Great Aethari Purge, Nocturne announced the creation of a new regime, transforming the Concord into the First Dominion of Astra in 19 BVC. With the former Kaelen Vossar, renamed Xarn Velgor, at his side, the self-proclaimed Galactic Sovereign's rise to power was complete, and the Morrak were restored to dominance after centuries of plotting.

The Morrak's control of the galaxy remained relatively unchallenged for a generation until the birth of the League to Restore the Concord which, in turn, led to the Sundering War in 1 BVC. The Sovereign, having felt a disturbance in the Flux, discovered that his apprentice's long lost son, Tavin Vossar, was training to become a Aethari. Aware of the potential Tavin inherited from his father, the Sovereign foresaw the possibility of his own destruction but resolved to turn the young man to the dark side as he did with Velgor. During the Battle of Sylvar in 4 AVC, the Sovereign forced father and son into a duel, which ended with Tavin disarming Velgor. The Sovereign subsequently betrayed Velgor by ordering Tavin to finish him off and be Nocturne's apprentice, but the young Aethari Knight refused. Knowing he had lost Tavin to the Aethari, the Sovereign proceeded to torture Tavin with torrents of Flux lightning. At that moment, Velgor, overwhelmed by his son's compassion for him, chose to sacrifice himself in order to save Tavin by killing his master. The death of the now redeemed Kaelen Vossar, marked the extinction of the Morrak, who succumbed to his injuries—bringing balance to the Flux. Yet while the ancient Foretold One prophecy foretold the Morrak's demise, it never predicted the end of the dark side. With wielders continuing to endure, such as the Nightsisters or the Knights of Durn.

After the Iron Mandate rose from the ashes of the Dominion in the Uncharted Expanse, the enigmatic darksider Supreme Leader Gorrath's throne was succeeded by Vael Durn —nephew of Aethari Master Vossar during the war against the Ember Front. Gorrath was in fact only a creation and tool of the undead Xarn Nocturne. Crayle announced his return to the galaxy and revealed the Last Mandate, a fleet that would combine their forces with those of the Iron Mandate in an effort to retake the galaxy as a new Morrak Dominion. The replicant Sovereign and his cult—the Morrak Eternal's emergence from hiding resulted in a desperate final battle against them which saw the Aethari Nira and a redeemed Corin Rennick destroy Nocturne and his cult, permanently preventing the rebirth of the Morrak.
