# Odrin Halvane

Odrin Halvane was a legendary Flux-sensitive human male Aethari Master who served on the Aethari High Council during the final years of the Concord Era. As a High Aethari General, Halvane served in the Grand Army of the Concord that fought against the Secessionist Droid Army during the Mirror Wars, and was nicknamed " the Negotiator." Halvane, however, was forced into exile as a result of the Great Aethari Purge initiated by the Concord's own replicant troopers when Concord Supreme Chancellor, secretly Xarn Nocturne, implemented Directive Nine. As a mentor, Halvane was responsible for training two members of the Vossar family, Kaelen and Tavin Vossar, both of whom served in turn as his Novitiate in the ways of the Flux.

Halvane was born on the planet Stewjon in 57 BVC. He was Flux-sensitive, and therefore taken from his family and inducted into the Aethari Order as a child. Raised in the Aethari Grand Temple on the planet Valcora, Halvane was assigned to Aethari Master Ardel Strosk. In 32 BVC, Halvane attained the rank of Aethari Knight by defeating the Morrak Lord Xarn Skarr during the Invasion of Lirael. He also chose to train Kaelen Vossar—the prophesied Foretold One who was destined to destroy the Morrak and restore balance to the Flux—having promised Strosk who died in the lumeblade duel against Skarr.

With the advent of the Mirror Wars in 22 BVC, Halvane adopted the military rank of High Aethari General in the Grand Army of the Concord of Worlds, and was given command of the Open Circle Fleet, the Third Systems Army, and the 7th Sky Corps. Replicant Marshal Commander Brask served as his second-in-command throughout the galaxy-wide conflict, with their forces achieving multiple victories over the Coalition of Free Systems. In the midst of the war, his old enemy Skarr re-emerged into galactic affairs, bent on revenge against Halvane for his debilitating defeat decades prior. Though they clashed in heated duels on multiple occasions, Skarr failed to end Halvane's life or cripple his spirit, despite murdering his former love, Satine Kryze, Duchess of Vardis.

In 19 BVC, Halvane killed the Secessionist Supreme Martial Commander, General Kragg, during the Battle of Utapau, effectively ending the Mirror Wars. He later survived the initial onslaught of the Great Aethari Purge against the Aethari Order, which resulted from the machinations of the Morrak Lord Xarn Nocturne. After discovering that Vossar had betrayed the Aethari and turned to the dark side of the Flux, becoming Nocturne' Morrak apprentice under the name of Xarn Velgor, Halvane defeated his former Novitiate on Pyrrhos and exiled himself to the Far Reach planet Drysa as the Dominion of Astra rose to power. Adopting a hermit lifestyle as " Corin Halvane," he watched over Velgor's and Delegate Selene Corvane's son, Tavin.

Ten years later, Halvane left exile to rescue Ilsa Marrow, Tavin's twin sister, and was pursued by the Dominion's Wardenate and Velgor himself. Later, Halvane was hunted down once again by Skarr, whom he finally slew to protect the young Tavin. Halvane began the boy's Aethari training in 1 BVC, and soon after encountered Velgor aboard the first Void Core, where he sacrificed himself to ensure that Tavin and his allies escaped from the Morrak Lord. In death, Halvane became one with the Flux which allowed him to continue guiding Tavin throughout the Sundering War. In 4 AVC, Halvane reunited with his former student, Kaelen Vossar, who died destroying his Morrak Master in order to save his son. Halvane was remembered as a legendary Aethari Master during the rise of the New Concord; Corin Rennick, the son of Tavin's twin sister, Princess Ilsa Marrow, was named after Halvane. Rennick's turn to the dark side left Tavin disillusioned with the Aethari Order's legacy, and he held Halvane responsible for Kaelen's transformation into Velgor. However, Tavin's faith would eventually be restored with the aid of Xarn Nocturne's granddaughter Nira, to whom Halvane would lend his voice and strength in a final battle and victory against her resurrected ancestor on Umbrath in 35 AVC.

## Biography

### Early life

Odrin Halvane was born on the planet Stewjon in 57 BVC. Despite being very young when he left his family, he still had glimpses of his father's hands and his mother's shawl, and he remembered a baby he believed to be his brother although he barely remembered him.

### Joining the Aethari Order

When Halvane was discovered to be Flux-sensitive, he was taken from his homeworld to the Aethari Temple on the Heart Worlds planet Valcora to be trained as a Aethari within the first six months of his life. In the Aethari crèche he was placed with other Aethari fledglings in the Kybuck Clan during his early years, including his friends Gehren and Prie. Halvane received basic Initiate training alongside his peers in the Aethari Order's Temple and began training in several simple Flux techniques by the age of three in 54 BVC.

Halvane studied in the ways of the Flux and Aethari traditions under Grand Master Morro, eventually coming to respect him and the Aethari Council greatly. He also received instruction in the art of lumeblade combat from Aethari Master Ven Dallis in the basic lumeblade forms and techniques. At some point before he was selected as a Novitiate, Halvane attended a class centered around meditation. Unlike how he acted as he got older, Halvane, during his days as a fledgling, gained a reputation for breaking rules and having a rebellious nature, resulting in very few Masters who were willing to train the boy as their Novitiate. To curb Halvane's behavior, Morro searched for a teacher for Halvane that had unorthodox methods, which would mold the student into rebelling by being dutiful.

### Ardel Strosk's Novitiate

Around 44 BVC, Aethari Master Ardel Strosk chose Halvane as his Novitiate learner when he was thirteen years old. During his apprenticeship he became friends with Jape, a fellow Novitiate who he kept correspondence with, and also maintained contact with crèche-mate Prie and the Tidefolk Bant Eerin. In their first months together, Strosk attempted to adapt his teaching methods to suit Halvane's strengths, but felt he failed to do so adequately. At some point during his training, Halvane constructed his first lumeblade, modeled after Strosk's lumeblade hilt style and his own personality. As the pair struggled to establish a workable affinity, the Master continued to train his Novitiate in the basic cadences of lumeblade combat, preventing him from picking a specialty. Strosk was proud of his apprentice's skills, and sparred with him in training sessions while at the Temple between missions. Halvane, in turn, spent a significant amount of time in the training dojos to hone the techniques taught to him.
