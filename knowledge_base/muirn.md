# Muirn

Muirn was a remote, unexplored planet in the Muirn system, and one of the purest places in the galaxy within the Flux. A remote world of swamps and forests, it served as a refuge for Aethari Grand Master Morro during his exile after the destruction of the Aethari Order. Tavin Vossar received advanced training in the ways of the Flux under Aethari Master Morro on Muirn, and it was later the place of Morro's death and transformation into the Flux.

## Description

Muirn was a planet located in the Sluis sector of the Far Reach Territories. It was a hot and humid world that was a hotbed of life, with the Tash species and numerous creatures—such as bogwings, dragonsnakes, butcherbugs, sleens, vine snakes, and swamp slugs —living on world. Aethari Master Morro would also make a home for himself on Muirn during his exile. Muirn was a mysterious, mist-shrouded, swampy planet, mostly covered in swamps and bogs, interspersed with countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multiple living caves. The continental and oceanic crusts of Muirn were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way of volcanic activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the lahdia plant, the yoghurt plant, gnarltree, and yarum seed. The planet was devoid of any cities.

Among the purest locations in the entire galaxy within the energy field known as the Flux, it shared a mysterious time-related quality with such locales as Mortis and Teyra. The Cave of Evil was a vergence aligned with the dark side of the Flux on Muirn. The Cave was able to expand its influence across Muirn until Morro's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the light side of the Flux that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Dominion Era, Muirn's ecosystem saw balance until Morro's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Muirn once more.

Muirn had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more.

## History

### Concord Era

During the High Concord Era, an academic paper was written that described Muirn as a world strong in the Flux.

During the Concord Era, Halka Four-Den Alpha Team was dispatched to the planet Muirn in order to chart and map the extending borders of the Concord of Worlds and learn more about it flora and fauna. After three months, Four-Den and her Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growing plants. No trace of the team was discovered.

Near the end of the Mirror Wars, Aethari Grand Master Morro traveled to the planet Muirn after hearing the voice of deceased Aethari Master Ardel Strosk. He ventured into the swamp, where he communed with Strosk, who appeared manifested as a group of fireflies. He told Morro of how to communicate with the living after death through the connection between the Living Flux and the Cosmic Flux. Then, Morro entered a cave, where he saw a vision of the Aethari fighting the replicants and then the mysterious Morrak Lord.

### Dominion Era

After his failure to defeat the Morrak Lord Xarn Nocturne following the formation of the Dominion of Astra, Morro exiled himself to Muirn. Using an E3-standard starship lifeboat, he began his exile, where he continued his studies of the Flux. The lifeboat served as Morro's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Muirn's swampy environment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Morro built a new home for himself. He positioned it near to the dark side cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years. However, the Muirn wet season created a need for the Aethari Master to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration.

At some point, former Sava Madelin Sun arrived on Muirn in search of Morro. After saving her from a monster, Morro used the Flux to lead Sun into the dark cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him. She then left the planet as Morro watched from afar.

Years later, Tavin Vossar of the League to Restore the Concord traveled to Muirn under the direction of late Aethari Master Odrin Halvane in hopes of finding Morro and being trained in the ways of the Aethari Order. Arriving with his astromech K7-T4, Vossar crashed into a bog upon landing and happened upon the Aethari, though he was unaware it was, in fact, Morro at the time. The old master took Vossar back to his hut for dinner, where he revealed his true identity to Vossar. Initially reluctant to train Vossar due to his age and lack of patience, Morro was eventually convinced to teach him by the Flux spirit of Halvane.

One night during Vossar's training, an Dominion probe droid from Project Swarm arrived on planet. The droid's operator, Maela, initially believed there was no reason to look further into the planet during the unit's patrol until she spotted Vossar's Lancer in the swamp. Although she realized the outline of what she saw resembled an Lancer, she had realized the cruelty of the Dominion after witnessing the aftermath of the Battle of Kryvel, so she deleted the footage before moving on to her next droid.

During his training, Vossar entered the Cave of Evil and confronted a vision of Xarn Velgor, who he struck down, only to see his own face in Velgor's mask. Later, Vossar ended his training early, however, once he sensed his friends Dace Rennick and Ilsa Marrow were in danger. Although Morro and Halvane attempted to reason with Vossar to stay, the student left the planet, promising to return and finish his training.

He would keep his word, and after learning Velgor was his father during their duel on Sky Haven, saving Ilsa and Grawlok from Velgor on Sky Haven, and rescuing Rennick from Hrask the Vogg on Drysa, he would return to Muirn to complete his Aethari training. Upon arriving, he found Morro sick and dying, who told him that his training was complete, but all he had to do was confront Velgor one last time, and the old master became one with the Flux. The ghost of Halvane then revealed to Vossar the truth that Ilsa was, in fact, a Vossar and Tavin's sister before Tavin left the planet to confront Velgor and the Sovereign in hopes of turning Velgor back to the light side. The Cave of Evil, meanwhile, took advantage of Morro's death to once again spread its power across Muirn.
