# Aethari Order

The Aethari Order, also known as the Order of the Aethari, and the New Aethari Order under Aethari Master Tavin Vossar, was a noble monastic and nontheistic religious order united in their devotion to the light side of the Flux. They served as mediators, warriors, and with teaching and exploratory efforts. With a history dating back thousands of years before the rise of the Dominion of Astra, the Aethari Knights —noted for their lumeblades and natural ability to harness the powers of the Flux —stood as the guardians of peace and justice in the Concord of Worlds. The Order was originally established on Teyra in the Uncharted Expanse. In later years, the Aethari Grand Temple on the Core World Valcora served as the hub of all Aethari activity in the galaxy. The Temple was also a training school for fledglings and Novitiates, who learned the ways of the Flux under the supervision of Aethari Masters, twelve of whom were elected to serve on the Aethari High Council —the Order's highest governing authority. The High Council's staff officers were the Grand Master, who functioned as the leader of the Order and was a title reserved for the wisest Aethari Master, and the Master of the Order, who served as the Council's appointed leader. The Aethari Code governed the Order's way of life; therefore, every Aethari was duty-bound to observe and uphold the Code or else risk expulsion from the Order.

The emergence of the Morrak Order was the result of a schism that fractured Aethari unity, dividing the Order between the Aethari who remained faithful to the light and a group of renegades that followed a rogue Aethari into exile for exploring the dark side of the Flux. Thenceforth, the Aethari and Morrak warred with each other for centuries. Although the last conflict concluded with the fall of the Old Concord, the ancient Morrak all but perished as well, allowing a new Concord of Worlds to rise under the protection of the Aethari Order. Unknown to the Aethari, however, the Morrak endured in secret for a millennium, beginning with Xarn Kesh, the sole-surviving Dark Lord of the Morrak who reformed the Morrak way by creating the Law of Pairs —a rule limiting the Morrak Order's ranks to two members: a Morrak Master to embody the power of the dark side, and a Morrak apprentice to covet that power. While the Aethari and Concord moved on, their ancient wars with the Morrak fading from memory, Kesh's legacy spawned a lineage of Morrak Lords that culminated in the rise of Xarn Nocturne approximately three decades before the Battle of Ostara.

During the final decades of the Concord, the Aethari Order vowed allegiance to the Galactic Assembly as the galaxy was beset by political instability and social unrest which, in turn, created a secessionist crisis that ultimately resulted in the formation of the Coalition of Free Systems. Lacking the sufficient manpower needed to protect the galaxy, the Aethari became commanders and generals in the newly-formed Grand Army of the Concord. For roughly three years they commanded armies of replicant troopers in a conflict known as the Mirror Wars, engaging the Confederacy's battle droid forces led by Count Varkon —a former Aethari Master who became a Morrak Lord —and the cybernetic warlord General Kragg. Though the Aethari fought to preserve the Concord, the government they served was secretly taken over by Nocturne who, as Delegate Aldus Crayle of Lirael, rose to power in the Assembly and became the last Supreme Chancellor of the Concord. The Aethari were unknowingly being deceived by the Morrak.

The Chancellor's deceptions and machinations culminated in the execution of Directive Nine, a secret protocol that forcibly turned the Aethari's replicant soldiers against them, leading to an all but total extermination of the Aethari Order. With most of the Aethari eliminated, Nocturne supplanted a thousand years of democracy with a new government known as the Dominion of Astra. In addition, Nocturne succeeded in manipulating Kaelen Vossar —the prophesied Foretold One who was destined to bring balance to the Flux by destroying the Morrak—to betray the Aethari and join the dark side, with Nocturne renaming him Xarn Velgor. As a Morrak Lord, the former Aethari Knight served the self-proclaimed Galactic Sovereign by hunting down the few surviving Aethari, who had gone into hiding across the galaxy. In spite of the Morrak's efforts, however, the Aethari continued to endure. Within a generation of the Dominion's rise to power, Odrin Halvane and Morro —both of whom survived the initial purge of the Order—trained Velgor's son, Tavin Vossar, in the Aethari arts. Tavin would later redeem his father, causing the redeemed Kaelen Vossar to return to the light and fulfill his destiny as the Foretold One, destroying both himself and his Master during the Battle of Sylvar.

The Dominion dissolved into various remnants as a result of their defeat in the Sundering War, allowing the League to Restore the Concord to rise as the New Concord. Under the new era, Tavin Vossar endeavored to rebuild the Order by training a new generation of Aethari. Among Vossar's students was his nephew, Corin Rennick, the son of his twin sister, Princess Ilsa Marrow, and the famed smuggler Dace Rennick. Corin, however, was seduced by the dark side of the Flux at the coaxing of Gorrath, an enigmatic figure who spearheaded the Dominion-inspired Iron Mandate. Tavin confronted and frightened Corin, who fought back and fled. As a result, Corin adopted the name and persona of Vael Durn, a Iron Mandate warlord and master of the Knights of Durn.

The new generation was betrayed and destroyed by Durn, who left the Aethari training temple burning by the time Vossar recovered from the attack. Feeling responsible for Corin's fall and the massacre of his apprentices, he went into exile and disappeared from the galaxy, leaving only traces of his location on a star map. The Iron Mandate and the Ember Front both sought to acquire the map, but ultimately it was the latter that succeeded due to the actions of the Saddar scavenger Nira. She followed the map to the planet Teyra, where the last Aethari had taken refuge in the first Aethari Temple.

Vossar, stricken by grief and believing his return would only cause more problems, was reluctant to train Nira or another generation of Aethari. However, the last Aethari eventually returned to the fight, buying time for the Ember Front to escape the siege of Saltmere led by Vael Durn, who became the new Supreme Leader after killing his master, Gorrath. Vossar became one with the Flux after exerting a great amount of energy to project himself across the galaxy to face his fallen nephew, but not before acknowledging that he would not be the last Aethari. His heroic tale spread across the galaxy, inspiring others and giving hope in a time of rising darkness.
