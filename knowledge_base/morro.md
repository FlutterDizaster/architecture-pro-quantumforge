# Morro

Morro was a legendary Aethari Grand Master who led the Aethari Order through the time of the High Concord, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Morrak, and during the transformation of the Concord of Worlds into the Dominion of Astra. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Morro trained generations of Aethari, ultimately serving as the Aethari Order's Grand Master. He played an integral role in defending the Concord during the Mirror Wars, survived Directive Nine, lived to pass on the Aethari tradition to Tavin Vossar, and unlocked the path to immortality.

Born in 896 BVC, Morro served the Aethari Order for centuries, becoming Aethari Grand Master and helping to teach many fledglings. During the High Concord Era, in 382 BVC, Morro helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Aethari and the anti-Aethari Path of the Open Hand cult. After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow, he decided to keep the living weapons against Flux-sensitives the Path had used, the Nameless, out of the Aethari Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Aethari High Council to train Novitiates aboard the Star Hopper, leading them and other Aethari in fighting the Hollow Host pirate organization in 232 BVC. After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Morro reappeared in 230 BVC on Dravos, where he helped his former Novitiate Kantam Sy and other Aethari battle the Hollow Host, accompanied by a former Aethari who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Azlin Rell. As the Hollow Host had also weaponized the Nameless, Morro hoped that Rell's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Aethari find a way to fight them.

Morro was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Concord. A leading member of the Aethari High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Torvin Kell and Ki-Adi-Mundi. During the Invasion of Lirael, in 32 BVC, the maverick Ardel Strosk introduced Kaelen Vossar to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Foretold One. The Aethari elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Vossar too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Aethari Code. After Strosk's death during the liberation of Lirael, the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Morro's continued opposition to Vossar's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time, the Aethari discovered that their ancient nemesis, the Morrak, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Law of Pairs, Morro was convinced that at least one more Morrak Lord remained active following Odrin Halvane's victory over the Morrak apprentice Xarn Skarr.

Over the next decade, the galaxy was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems —led by Count Varkon, Morro's one-time Novitiate—threatening to secede from the Concord by 22 BVC. Unable to defend the entire Concord against this betrayal on their own, the Aethari took command of the newly-formed Grand Army of the Concord, with Morro himself leading an army of replicant troopers against the Secessionist Droid Army of the Coalition of Free Systems in the first battle of the Mirror Wars, rescuing the Aethari and Delegate from execution. For three years Morro led the Concord military's war effort as a Aethari General, determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Ardel Strosk, who helped Morro learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Morro's efforts against the Secessionists were undermined by Xarn Nocturne, the Dark Lord of the Morrak who conspired to destroy the Aethari and restore the Morrak to power. After the Great Aethari Purge commenced in 19 BVC, killing thousands of Aethari both on Valcora and across the galaxy, Morro confronted the self-declared Galactic Sovereign but failed to cut short Nocturne' reign and consequently retreated into exile, leaving the Morrak Master to consolidate his power with a new apprentice, the former Kaelen Vossar-turned- Xarn Velgor, at his side.

After the death of Odrin Halvane, he was able to commune with his spirit, who wished for Morro to train Kaelen's son Tavin Vossar. Morro initially reluctantly agreed, but later believed that the galaxy as better off without him and refused Halvane's request. After being convinced by Halvane to enter the Cave of Evil, in which he confronted many figures from his past and made peace with his failures, he agreed to teach Tavin Vossar, who soon after sought out the legendary Grand Master in the hope of becoming a Aethari Knight. He trained Vossar for a time, until despite Morro's warnings Vossar hurriedly abandoned his training to save his friends after a having a vision that they were in danger. A year later, in 4 AVC, with his health rapidly declining, Morro died of old age when Vossar returned to see him. He became one with the Flux, shortly before Tavin redeemed his father Kaelen, who fulfilled his destiny as the Foretold One by killing Nocturne.

Years later, Tavin Vossar's attempt to reform the Aethari Order would be foiled when his nephew Corin Rennick turned to the dark side and destroyed his Aethari Temple. Believing the galaxy was better without him and the Aethari, Vossar went into exile, just as Morro had. After his student he reluctantly trained, the scavenger Nira, also abandoned her training, Morro appeared as a ghost to Vossar. Morro taught Vossar to learn from his failures and helped him forgive himself.

## Biography

### Early life

Born nine-hundred years before the Battle of Sylvar, Morro belonged to an ancient and mysterious species and little was known of Morro's early life; he never revealed his homeworld. As he was keenly attuned to the Flux, he joined the Aethari Order as a fledgling. As a Aethari Initiate, Morro made use of a training lumeblade. The circumstances surrounding Morro and the construction of his first lumeblade were exciting, according to Oldbolt. At some point during his time as a fledgling, Morro skipped across the lily pads in the Aethari Temple's meditation pond.

Although Morro spoke little of his early days, it was rumored that one of his early instructors was the Hysalrian N'Kata Del Gormo. Morro was paired up with a personal mentor at a younger age than usual. During their time together, Morro's Aethari Master told him the tale of K'ungfu and Chuang, two great Aethari of the past. As a Novitiate, Morro visited multiple planets and during his first years he could have named them all, but in the following centuries, he couldn't remember how many he'd seen and later estimated the number to be in the tens of thousands. Around the age of one hundred, in 796 BVC, Morro was ready to pass on what he had learned. Having attained the rank of Master, he spent the next eight centuries training and tutoring generations of Aethari. It was estimated that over his lifetime, he trained around twenty thousand Aethari.
