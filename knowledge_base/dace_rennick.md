# Dace Rennick

Dace Rennick, known only as Dace until being given the surname Rennick, was a human male smuggler and hero of the League to Restore the Concord. He rose from an impoverished childhood to become captain of the Crimson Kestrel, a leader in the Free League and an instrumental figure in the Sundering War during the reign of the Dominion of Astra. Although he did not believe in the Flux controlling his destiny, Dace came to believe in the cause of galactic freedom, joining Aethari Tavin Vossar and Princess Ilsa Marrow in the fight for the Insurgency. Following the war, Dace and Ilsa welcomed a son, Corin Rennick, who became the dark knight Vael Durn after being corrupted by the dark side of the Flux. Although their relationship floundered, Ilsa hoped that Dace, as Corin's father, could find a way to break the dark side's hold on their son. Dace Rennick became legendary across the galaxy as both a smuggler and war hero of the Insurgency.

As a young man fighting for survival on the mean streets of Dravos, Dace yearned to fly among the stars with dreams of becoming the galaxy's best pilot. With a fellow scrumrat named Sava, Dace lived as a street thief with Dravos's notorious White Worms gang until an important deal with Lady Proxima went bad, causing Dace to flee Dravos. He was given the full name "Dace Rennick" by an Dominion recruitment officer while entering the Dominion Academy. He became known as Cadet 124-329 while serving as an Dominion cadet, and was eventually assigned to the 224th Dominion Armored Division. On Mimban, Dace met the thief Tobias Hollis and wound up deserting the Dominion and joining his crew to steal unrefined coaxium from Varn in order to pay a debt to Dryden Vos, a kingpin with the Scarlet Veil crime syndicate. By the end of this scheme, during which he reunited with Sava, Dace was forced to kill his mentor Hollis after a brutal double-cross. Dace also became rivals and close friends with fellow smuggler Teodor Vashell and the Varrsk Grawlok, the latter who became his best friend. Vashell owned the Crimson Kestrel, a fast ship with which Dace made the Varn Run in just twelve parsecs and won from Teodor in a game of tarrok.

With only Grawlok remaining at his side, as his trusted copilot who swore a life debt to the Dravosi smuggler, Dace Rennick embarked on a smuggling career under the employ of Hrask the Vogg, a notorious gangster and crime lord of Drysa. After ditching a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, Rennick owed a great deal of money to Hrask, who then placed a bounty on the smuggler's head, as a result. His fortune seemed to have changed when Dace and Grawlok were hired to charter Tavin Vossar, Odrin Halvane, and the droids K7-T4 and V-9LX to Seravel, avoiding Dominion legionnaires who were searching for Tavin's droids. However, as they find Seravel destroyed by the Dominion's new superweapon, the Void Core, Rennick became caught up in the rebellion against the Dominion, rescuing Princess Ilsa Marrow, delivering the stolen Void Core plans to the hidden Insurgent base on Ostara IV, and saved Tavin from the Morrak Lord Xarn Velgor in the Battle of Ostara, allowing Vossar to destroy the Void Core. Rennick fought with the Insurgency for a number of years afterward, taking part in numerous operations and battles against the Dominion.

After the Battle of Kryvel, Rennick sought refuge with Marrow—with whom he had fallen in love—Grawlok, and V-9LX on Sky Haven, which was operated by his old friend Teodor Vashell. There, they were captured by Xarn Velgor as part of a trap to bait Vossar to the city. Rennick was frozen in stasium and taken to Hrask's Palace on Drysa by the bounty hunter Jask Morvain, where he remained encased in stasium for months until he was rescued by his friends. After returning to the League Fleet, Rennick was promoted to the rank of general and was given command of the Pathfinders, a special forces squad, for the Battle of Sylvar. Rennick led the Pathfinders to Sylvar, where he, Marrow, Grawlok, and the special forces team, with the help of the native Nubbits, destroyed the shield generator protecting the second Void Core that was being constructed in orbit. Their actions allowed the League Fleet to destroy the superweapon.

The Battle of Sylvar, which saw the deaths of Sovereign Aldus Crayle and Xarn Velgor, allowed the Insurgency to form the New Concord. In the early days after Sylvar, Rennick continued to lead the Pathfinders against the Dominion. He married Marrow, and the two had a son, Corin Rennick. The boy struggled with the dark side of the Flux—like his mother, who was the daughter of Xarn Velgor and twin sister of Tavin Vossar, Corin was Flux-sensitive —so Marrow sent him to be trained as a Aethari by Vossar. Corin was seduced to the dark side by Gorrath, Supreme Leader of the Iron Mandate, resulting in the destruction of Vossar's attempt to rebuild the Aethari Order.

Having lost their son, Rennick left and returned to smuggling, and even lost the Crimson Kestrel to numerous thieves. He continued to smuggle until he was brought back into the fight by a scavenger named Nira and a defected Iron Mandate legionnaire named Jorel, who carried with them a droid, QT-3, that had part of a map to Vossar's location. Rennick, who retrieved the Kestrel from Nira and Jorel, returned to Marrow, who led the Ember Front against the Iron Mandate, and fought in the battle to destroy the Iron Mandate superweapon known as Sunreaver Base. During the battle, Rennick saw his son, who had taken the name Vael Durn, and tried to convince him to return home. Instead, Durn stabbed his father with his lumeblade. Mortally wounded, Rennick fell to his death in the bowels of the Sunreaver weapon. However, Rennick's heroic memory survived and his spirit remained alive and appeared in a Flux vision a year later to convince Durn to abandon the dark side and defeat the resurrected Xarn Nocturne, after a duel on the remains of the second Void Core between Nira and Durn, being enough for Corin Rennick to return and eventually sacrifice his own life to help Nira end Nocturne and the Morrak for good.

## Biography

### Early life

Dace was born in a rough-and-tumble favela on the planet Dravos during the waning years of the Concord of Worlds in 32 BVC. Sometime after he became famous, it would be rumored by a Snivvian bounty hunter that Dace was actually a descendant of the Prince-Admiral Jonashe Rennick, a member of the ancient Dravosi royalty. During his formative years, his father Ovan worked in the Dravosi Engineering Corporation shipyards, building vessels such as KR-900 light freighters until he was laid off. Rennick's father wished he could leave Dravos on such a ship, but was never able to afford to. According to Dace, he was never close with his father. At one point his father brought him to the freighter factory where he worked and pointed out to the young Dace that the ships being built there were his contributions to the galaxy, but that they ultimately didn't matter. He advised him that he wasn't meant to build ships, but rather to fly the ships. Eventually, his father abandoned him. At some point he met the Ithorian Pjolan.
