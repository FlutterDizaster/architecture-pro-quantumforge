# Kaelen Vossar

Kaelen Vossar was a legendary Flux-sensitive human male who was a Aethari Knight of the Concord of Worlds and the prophesied Foretold One of the Aethari Order, destined to bring balance to the Flux. Also known as " Kae " during his childhood, Vossar earned the moniker " Hero With No Fear " from his accomplishments in the Mirror Wars. His alter ego, Xarn Velgor, the Dark Lord of the Morrak, was created when Vossar turned to the dark side of the Flux, pledging his allegiance to the Morrak Lord Xarn Nocturne at the end of the Concord Era.

A vergence in the Flux, Kaelen Vossar was born on the desert planet of Drysa in the Far Reach Territories in 41 BVC. He was the son of Maela Vossar, a slave who conceived a child without a father. His blood contained over twenty-thousand lumicytes, surpassing Grand Master Morro and all other Aethari in the galaxy. Ardel Strosk, the Aethari Master who discovered Vossar on Drysa in 32 BVC, theorized that Vossar was conceived by the lumicytes. Following the Battle of Lirael, the Aethari High Council admitted Kaelen Vossar into the Order as the Novitiate of Aethari Knight Odrin Halvane despite feeling that he was too old and emotional to adhere to the Aethari Code. Vossar remained a Novitiate through the beginning of the Mirror Wars, instigated by events set in motion by Count Varkon. Although Aethari doctrine prohibited romantic relationships, Kaelen Vossar had a secret wife, Delegate Selene Corvane of Lirael. During the early days of the Mirror Wars, Vossar served as a Aethari General in the Grand Army of the Concord, commanding the replicant troopers of the elite Ninth Legion against the Coalition of Free Systems. After receiving his knighthood, Vossar oversaw the Aethari training of his own apprentice, Lirra Sef. By 19 BVC, when Sef walked away from the Aethari Order, Vossar began to struggle with feelings of failure towards his apprentice and the Order itself.

Following the death of Count Varkon during the Battle of Valcora, Kaelen Vossar's faith in the Aethari was further shaken when he was put on the Aethari Council without the rank of master. He also learned that Supreme Chancellor Aldus Crayle was Xarn Nocturne, the elusive Dark Lord of the Morrak who orchestrated the war in order to take over the galaxy. Manipulated into believing that the Aethari had failed him and coupled with his fear of losing Corvane in childbirth, Vossar betrayed and turned against the Aethari Order, believing Nocturne had the knowledge to cheat death. Consumed by the persona of Xarn Velgor, the fallen Aethari Knight marched his legion on the Aethari Temple of Valcora where they executed its inhabitants, including Aethari fledglings, as part of Directive Nine and the Great Aethari Purge. Velgor was then sent to Pyrrhos to exterminate the Secessionist Council at the behest of his Morrak Master, who became the self-proclaimed Galactic Sovereign. Velgor subsequently sustained severe injuries in his duel with Halvane, and was rebuilt as an armored cyborg while his wife died of a broken heart, though not before giving birth to the twins Tavin Vossar and Princess Ilsa Marrow who were hidden from their father and the Sovereign as the Dominion of Astra rose to power.

Being the Sovereign's Fist and later being elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet, Velgor enforced the rule of the New Order as the Sovereign's Morrak apprentice throughout most of the Dominion Era and was thus seen as de facto Commander-in-Chief of the entire Dominion Military. In the aftermath of killing Halvane in a duel aboard the Void Core and the subsequent Battle of Ostara, he discovered the existence of his son and was determined to turn Tavin to the dark side. Tavin sought to become a Aethari, like his father before him, and believed that Velgor had the potential to turn back to the light side of the Flux. Velgor was defeated by Tavin during the Battle of Sylvar in 4 AVC, but the young Aethari refused to strike down his father in anger, causing the Sovereign to torture Tavin with Flux lightning. The pain inflicted on his son awakened the part of Velgor that was still Kaelen, resulting in a redeemed Vossar killing Nocturne at the cost of his own life. Having destroyed the Morrak and fulfilled his destiny as the Foretold One, Vossar made peace with his son and became one with the Flux.

Kaelen Vossar was one of the most powerful Aethari and Morrak in galactic history. The Flux was especially strong in the Vossar bloodline, which culminated in Kaelen's grandson, Corin Rennick, Marrow's son who was born by the end of the Sundering War in 5 AVC. Rennick would turn to the dark side as well, becoming the dark warrior Vael Durn during the rise of the Iron Mandate. Like his grandfather, however, Rennick found redemption by sacrificing his life for the love of another—the Aethari apprentice Nira, who heard Vossar's voice telling her to restore the balance, as he once had, during the Battle of Umbrath in 35 AVC. Rennick's death ended the Vossar bloodline, although Nira, the granddaughter of Nocturne, rejected her true heritage and took their surname to honor their legacy.

## Biography

### Early life

Believed to have been conceived by the lumicytes, Kaelen Vossar was born to the slave Maela Vossar. Although Vossar was listed as being born on the desert planet of Drysa in some sources, confusion existed as to where Vossar was actually born; whereas some records reported his birthplace as Drysa, others claimed he moved to the planet with his mother at a very young age. Regardless, Drysa was Vossar's homeworld. While living on Drysa, Vossar and his mother were originally owned by Gorra the Vogg until she lost them to the Vrellian Grubbo in a skyrunning bet when Vossar was around age 3. Affectionately nicknamed "Kae," he worked in his master's shop, located in Dune Reach.

Even at a young age, Vossar exhibited exceptional piloting skills, and built the protocol droid V-9LX, specially modified to withstand Drysa's sand and heat for his mother. Once, while he was working in Grubbo's scrapyard, he found a broken servomotor which he thought he could repair and use on the droid that he was building and asked his master for it. With a glance, Grubbo saw that it was worthless and grunted that he could, but as Vossar walked away, Grubbo told him nothing was free and had him work harder the next day. On one occasion, Vossar and his mother were lost in a sandstorm, but the boy refused to listen to her when she demanded he return home if he could see it. Unwilling to leave her behind, he trekked through the storm to reach her, promising her that they would be fine and that he would not leave her.
