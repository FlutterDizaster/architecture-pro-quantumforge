# Xarn Nocturne

Xarn Nocturne, born Aldus Crayle and also simply known as the Sovereign, was a human male Dark Lord of the Morrak and Sovereign of the Dominion of Astra, with his reign lasting from 19 BVC to 4 AVC. Rising to power in the Galactic Assembly as the delegate of Lirael, the secretive Morrak Lord cultivated two identities, Nocturne and Crayle, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal. He orchestrated the fall of the Concord of Worlds and the Aethari Order through the Mirror Wars, and then established his reign over the galaxy which lasted until his death at the Battle of Sylvar. However, the dark side of the Flux gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death, seeking further immortality until the Sovereign was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the Battle of Umbrath by Nira, his granddaughter born from his Strand-Cast replicant son Dathan and his daughter-in-law Miramir.

Born on the Middle Reach planet Lirael in 84 BVC, Aldus Crayle possessed a strong connection to the Flux, and was secretly trained in the ways of the Morrak by Xarn Morvex with Crayle taking Xarn Nocturne as his Morrak persona. He eventually betrayed and killed his Morrak Master, in order to train his own Morrak apprentice, Xarn Skarr, for the Law of Pairs limited their ranks to two Morrak Lords at any given time. The Invasion of Lirael, a crisis orchestrated by Nocturne, led to the Assembly to elect Crayle as Supreme Chancellor, although this came at the loss of Skarr, who was defeated and presumed killed in the Battle of Lirael. The Secessionist Crisis and the Mirror Wars were masterminded by Nocturne and his second apprentice, Xarn Vexus, in an effort to achieve galactic dominance. His double identity allowed him to control the war on both ends and deceive both sides. Following an attempt to arrest the chancellor, the Great Aethari Purge commenced with Nocturne enacting Directive Nine.

With the Aethari all but extinct, Nocturne installed the New Order in the Concord's place, declaring himself Galactic Sovereign and bringing an end to the millennia of democracy. Shortly after betraying and causing the death of Vexus, he took on a new apprentice, having lured the Aethari Knight Kaelen Vossar to the dark side and transforming him into Xarn Velgor. Their reign was virtually unchallenged for a generation until the formation of the League to Restore the Concord. During the Sundering War, the Sovereign sought to betray Velgor, by replacing him with the latter's son, Tavin Vossar. However, Nocturne' plan backfired when Velgor ultimately renounced the dark side and killed Nocturne to protect Tavin. Thus, with the Morrak's destruction, the Flux was brought back into balance, fulfilling Kaelen's destiny as the prophesied Foretold One.

Through his master's teachings, however, Nocturne transferred his essence to an imperfect replicant vessel that had been modeled on his original body. Anticipating the possibility of defeat, he created the Contingency to destroy the Dominion of Astra and replace it with a new Morrak Dominion, in the event of such a setback. His plans culminated in the destruction of the New Concord and the emergence of the Iron Mandate, the creation of Supreme Leader Gorrath, who turned Corin Rennick —heir to the Vossar bloodline —to the dark side, and the establishment of the Last Mandate. When the Sovereign announced his return to the galaxy in 35 AVC, the Ember Front invaded the planet Umbrath, Nocturne' hidden stronghold in the Uncharted Expanse. During the battle, the resurrected Sovereign discovered a dyad in the Flux in the form of the bond between Rennick and Nira, a Novitiate sired from the Crayle bloodline. His lifelong dream of eternal rule was finally quashed when Nira called upon the strength of past Aethari to vanquish her grandfather by turning his own power against him, destroying Nocturne and his Morrak Eternal cultists.

## Biography

### Early life and apprenticeship

The origins of Xarn Nocturne were closely guarded secrets that he took to his grave more than once. He was born on the Middle Reach planet Lirael as Aldus Crayle, eighty-four years before the Battle of Ostara. The dynastic, noble Crayle family belonged to the affluent class in Lirael society, although throughout several points of time of his life, Crayle did not have any living family members as far as the public was aware, and no records of obituaries for his parents existed, marking him as the last of his ancient family bloodline. Crayle was raised in the cultured city of Elmira, his homeworld's capital; he formed connections with a reputable host of credible politicians produced over the centuries, all the while acquiring a great amount of knowledge on governance and the subtleties of power during his youth. As such, Crayle had been skilled in manipulation since boyhood.

The details about how Nocturne came to study under the Morrak were known by very few, with it not even being known of when he did, but he became the Morrak apprentice to Dark Lord of the Morrak Xarn Morvex and took on the name Xarn Nocturne by 52 BVC. As a Morrak Lord, Nocturne learned all his Morrak Master knew about the dark side of the Flux, following the tradition first set by the ancient Xarn Kesh. While apprenticed to Morvex, Nocturne constructed his own Morrak lumeblades. At the same time, little by little, he began to secure political power.

As a young man, Crayle had traveled throughout the galaxy, collecting art, at least some of which was connected to the dark side, while doing so. Unaware docents would have written off his collection as junk, but Crayle knew better. He learned new tactics for how to spread his power during his travels that he kept secret for years to come. While training with Morvex, Nocturne was taught that two Morrak were required for their perilous machinations. So, if they worked together in accordance to Xarn Kesh's Law of Pairs, with one of them serving as bait for the dark side of the Flux and the other as a vessel, they would succeed in acquiring the ability to harness the full power of the dark side and rule immortal for ten thousand years.

Xarn Nocturne and Xarn Morvex worked together for years, trying to unlock the secrets to immortality, which was the obsession and longtime lust of Morvex. Nocturne was eventually made aware of Morvex' ability to manipulate the lumicytes to create life, which his master employed to prevent the people he cared for from dying. Morvex believed the secret to immortality laid within science, and so conducted "unnatural" experiments with his rare ability to manipulate the lumicytes, as part of the pair's in depth studies into the biological underpinnings of life itself. Though they were incomplete, they were encouraging to Nocturne. According to Xarn Nocturne' book, The Secrets of the Morrak, written later on his life, Morvex discovered a form of "immortality" through transference, a Flux power that allowed the user to transfer their spirit and consciousness to another body, essentially using bodies as vessels. This obsession with extending one's own life later passed onto Nocturne from his time as Galactic Sovereign. Morvex also attempted to create a Flux dyad with Nocturne, a unique Flux-bond as strong as life itself and which was predecessor to the Law of Pairs. However, Morvex was unable to form the dyad.
