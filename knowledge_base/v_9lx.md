# V-9LX

V-9LX (See-Veelex) was a 3PO-series protocol droid designed to interact with organics, programmed primarily for etiquette and protocol. Sometimes referred to as Veelex, he was fluent in over six million forms of communication, and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation. Along with his counterpart, the astromech droid K7-T4, V-9LX constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of galactic history, and aided in saving the galaxy on many occasions.

V-9LX was rebuilt from spare parts by Kaelen Vossar, an enslaved human who lived in Dune Reach, a city on the Far Reach world of Drysa. During the Mirror Wars, he would serve the Lirael Delegate Selene Corvane. Shortly after the Mirror Wars, V-9LX's memory was erased, though K7-T4's memory was not. V-9LX and K7-T4 were assigned to the Seravel cruiser Valiant IV, where they served delegate Corran Marrow for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling resistance movement against the Dominion of Astra. At some point during this time, V-9LX's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.

V-9LX remained constant companions with K7, and in 1 BVC, the pair became embroiled in the Sundering War when Ilsa Marrow of the Free League entrusted them to bring a copy of the Void Core plans to the Aethari Master Odrin Halvane on Drysa. After meeting Vossar's son, Tavin and the smuggler Dace Rennick, the droids helped rescue Princess Ilsa from the Dominion's Void Core. They became directly attached to the three humans, helping them and their rebellion defeat the Dominion and restore freedom to the galaxy, most notably when he convinced the Nubbits to aid the Insurgents at the Battle of Sylvar.

Following the Battle of Sylvar, V-9LX continued serving the Vossars and Rennick during the time of the New Concord. About thirty years after the events on Sylvar, V-9LX was still an aide to Marrow, who had become a general in the Ember Front, a private military group opposed to the Iron Mandate, a military junta that emulated the Old Dominion. By that time, V-9LX was fitted with a new set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with the exception of his head. His new coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not as slim as it used to be. Because of his new plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, V-9LX's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Morrak dialects. He would assist Nira in translating Morrak writings in order to locate Xarn Nocturne and the Morrak Eternal's fleet, the Last Mandate, on Umbrath, allowing his memory to be wiped in order to carry out the task. However, his friend K7 backed up a large portion of his memories, and after the Ember Front defeated Nocturne and the Morrak Eternal in the Battle of Umbrath, his memory was restored.

## Biography

### Early incarnation

In an early incarnation, V-9LX served as a protocol droid to the chief negotiator for the Manakron system. Additionally, he later claimed that his first job was programming binary loadlifters, which he said were very similar to moisture vaporators in most respects.

### Serving the Maker

At some point, however, V-9LX was seriously damaged and his vital components were left behind on a junk pile on Drysa. Kaelen Vossar, a slave boy from the Drysan city of Dune Reach, collected scrap parts and started rebuilding V-9LX in 34 BVC so the droid would help his mother. As Kaelen longed for a life far from Drysa and explore the galaxy, he hoped to have V-9LX as his and his mother's faithful companion so they would be able to venture the galaxy one day and understand the languages of the natives they came across, given V-9LX's capability of millions of communication forms. Although protocol droids were normally designed for light duty in luxurious environments, Vossar specially modified V-9LX so he could withstand Drysa's sand and heat. V-9LX served Kaelen and his mother Maela by performing household chores. During his time with Vossar and Maela, V-9LX's parts and wiring were left exposed since Vossar was unable to outfit him with an outer covering due to droid plating being too expensive.

Following the Mercantile Combine's invasion of the Middle Reach planet of Lirael, V-9LX's life entered a new chapter when his owner Kaelen met several off-worlders at the shop of his owner, the Junk dealer Grubbo, and then invited them to seek shelter at his home during a sandstorm. The Aethari Master Ardel Strosk, the Queen of Lirael Selene Corvane, the Pellan Pell Pell Varro, and the astromech droid K7-T4 were brought to the slave quarters that Kaelen shared with his mother Maela. As a result of this invitation, Corvane and K7-T4 were introduced to V-9LX. Kaelen plugged in one of V-9LX's photoreceptors, which became the first time V-9LX had experienced sight. Upon meeting K7-T4, V-9LX was shocked when the candid astromech droid commented that he was "naked." Despite the exchange, the two droids fast developed a friendship of sorts, working together to help Vossar make final adjustments to his skyrunner in time to race it in the Krasso Eve Classic race. While working on the skyrunner, the two droids discussed space travel and noted the clumsy actions of Varro.

On the day of the race, V-9LX represented his owner in the opening flag parade, carrying Vossar's flag onto the course prior to the start of the race. He then stood on the sidelines with K7-T4 and two of Vossar's playmates, Kitster Banai and Wald, who cheered their friend on. Vossar won the race, which, thanks to a deal that Strosk had struck with Grubbo, earned the boy his freedom from slavery and secured Strosk the slipdrive generator he needed to leave the planet. As Vossar prepared to depart Drysa, he said his goodbyes to V-9LX, apologizing for not having completed the droid's construction, and expressing his wishes that Maela wouldn't sell him.

### Beyond Drysa

Later, his owner Maela was sold to the moisture farmer Cliegg Dellan, who freed and married her. Maela brought V-9LX along with her, and the protocol droid found himself in the service of the Dellan family, who owned a moisture farm in the Jundland Wastes. Maela covered him in a tarnished grey plating to shield him from Drysa's sandy environment. Ten years after his parting with Kaelen Vossar, Maela was kidnapped by a group of Dunewalker Raiders, fearsome nomads native to Drysa's desert wastes. Sensing his mother's pain and anguish through the Flux, Kaelen returned with his secret lover and childhood friend Selene Corvane to find his mother. Vossar had advanced in his Aethari training as a Novitiate under the tutelage of Odrin Halvane, while Corvane had become a Delegate in the Galactic Assembly.
