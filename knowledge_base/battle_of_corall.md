# Battle of Corall

The Battle of Corall was the first major battle of the Sundering War which was fought in 1 BVC between the League to Restore the Concord and the Dominion of Astra. The battle was preceded by years of attacks by the rebel cells against the Dominion and began the five-year period in which the League successfully fought to replace the Dominion with the New Concord.

Prior to the battle, Free League Intelligence Service intercepted information suggesting that renowned scientist Orrin Halden was building an Dominion superweapon capable of destroying entire planets. League leadership sent Orrin Halden's daughter, Mira Halden, and Captain Dorin Sorrel to learn more about her father's involvement, and their subsequent efforts saw the League discover the existence of the Void Core, as well as a fatal flaw in the design placed there by Orrin Halden.

Though the League High Command refused to openly oppose the Dominion, Halden and Sorrel led a small unit of the Marine Corps and other allies and defied orders, taking it upon themselves to launch an incursion of the Corall Citadel, as the rebels planned to infiltrate the heavily guarded Citadel Tower base on Corall to retrieve the Void Core plans. Under the call sign Ember Squad, the unit infiltrated Corall's planetary shield in a stolen Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle and proceeded to wage a guerrilla assault on the Citadel's outskirts, drawing away Dominion forces so that Sorrel, Halden, and the droid R-7KX could infiltrate the facility. Director Edric Varsh, the former commander of the Void Core, attempted to stop Halden and Sorrel from stealing the plans, but the heavily outnumbered Insurgents received support from the League Fleet under Admiral Halloran, whose forces engaged the Dominion Dreadhulls above Corall and worked to destroy the Shield Gate protecting the planet.

With the Shield Gate's destruction, Ember Squad was able to transmit the stolen plans to the fleet above, though all remaining Insurgent ground forces were eradicated by a low-powered blast from the newly arrived Void Core that destroyed the Citadel and surrounding area. The arrival of forces directed by the Dark Lord of the Morrak, Xarn Velgor, made short work of the rebel ships that did not manage to retreat, but Princess Ilsa Marrow escaped the battle in the Valiant IV; Lord Velgor pursued her to the planet Drysa and captured her there, but the plans were already out of his reach. The plans eventually found their way to the League, who used them to pinpoint the Void Core's weakness and destroy it at the Battle of Ostara.

## Prelude

### Revelation on Ashar

Orrin Walton Halden, a aurite crystal expert, was recruited by Edric Varsh, who served as the Director of Dominion Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research for the Dominion of Astra, to Project Celestial Power, which Varsh claimed to be a project to provide unlimited energy. Halden soon discovered his research was being weaponized to create a superweapon, the Void Core, and escaped to the remote world of Lah'mu. In 13 BVC, Varsh, frustrated by the stalled work, located Halden and forcibly returned him to the project after killing his wife Lyra. However Halden's daughter Mira escaped and joined Garrick Dravin's Partisans before being abandoned at the age of 16. Halden, although forced to continue research, put a subtle weakness into the the design of the Void Core that could destroy it completely.

In 1 BVC, as the construction on the Void Core neared its completion, Orrin began to fear for what would happen if the superweapon was unleashed upon the galaxy. He gave a message to Tamsin Fenwick, an imperial cargo pilot who wished to defect, and instructed him to give the message to the League, telling him to seek out Garrick Dravin, an old friend of his who lived on Ashar. Dravin, however, had grown increasingly paranoid and doubted that Fenwick's defection was sincere, subjecting him to torture (via Bor Gullet) and imprisoning him.

Through contacts in the Partisans, League intelligence operative Dorin Sorrel learned of Orrin's message and Dravin's capture of Fenwick. The now two-year-old Free League then recruited Mira to convince Dravin to turn it over to them. Accompanying Sorrel and his droid R-7KX, she went to Ashar and was captured by the Partisans during an ambush on Dominion forces in Ashar City. With them were captured Guardians of the Seers Sennet Arvo and Holt Garran. Mira met Dravin, who eventually showed her the message.

In the message, Orrin explained that he had purposely designed the Void Core's reactor module to be unstable so that any kind of pressurized explosion to the module would set off a chain reaction that would destroy the station completely, but in order to locate it, they'd need a full set of plans, which were located in the data vault of the Citadel Tower on Corall. Before the message could finish and Mira could physically take it with her, the Void Core arrived and destroyed Ashar City, killing Dravin and forcing Mira, Sorrel and their allies to withdraw.

They then set off for the planet Eadu, where Orrin was headquartered, to bring him to the League so he could help them himself. Varsh, suspecting Orrin to have sent the message, arrived on Eadu to confront him. Sorrel, who had secret orders to eliminate Orrin, relented on the verge of killing him. At that moment, an League starfighter squadron arrived and bombarded the Dominion complex where Orrin worked, killing him and forcing Varsh to retreat. Sorrel, Mira and their team returned to Ostara-4 on a stolen Dominion cargo shuttle.

### Formation of Ember Squad

After returning from Eadu, Mira and Captain Sorrel participated in an League Council meeting, where they told the gathered beings what they had learned about the Void Core, and that they needed to get the plans held at Corall. Most were skeptical that such a weapon existed, and those that believed it did were unconvinced that it had a deliberate weakness on account of the fact that the news came from a criminal who was the daughter of an Dominion engineer-and thought that it would be better to hide or surrender. Disheartened, Halden walked away from the meeting, only to find that Sorrel and many others—including Sennet Arvo, Holt Garran, and Tamsin Fenwick—were still willing to go with her and fight. Together, they called themselves " Ember Squad " and undertook an unsanctioned mission to Corall using the Dominion cargo shuttle that had been stolen earlier, and headed to the planet.

Once there, Fenwick transmitted the shuttle's clearance codes, which got them through the planetary shield and onto the surface. Around the same time, Varsh also returned to Corall in order to have the command staff scour through any and all Void Core–related project files made by Orrin Halden. Director Varsh's motive was to see if there were any irregularities after the Dark Lord of the Morrak, Xarn Velgor, who had castigated him for compromising the Void Core's secrecy with his destruction of Ashar City, told the officer that he could only earn his support and Sovereign Crayle's favor if he proved that Halden didn't reveal anything regarding the Void Core's weaknesses to the Insurgency.
