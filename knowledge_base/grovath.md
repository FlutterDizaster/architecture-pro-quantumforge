# Grovath

Grovath, also known as Planet Varrsk C to some humans in the Heart Worlds, was a wroshyr tree-covered forest planet located in the southwestern quadrant of the galaxy and the homeworld of the Varrsk species. Four millennia before the Battle of Ostara, Grovath was discovered by the Varrum Corporation, who enslaved the Varrsk population and renamed the planet G5-623, later to Edean. Using superior technology, the company managed to enslave the Varrsks until an uprising drove the oppressors away. During the Mirror Wars, Grovath was a member of the Concord of Worlds, and endured enslavement under the Dominion of Astra, the Concord's successor. Later, during the rise of the New Concord, Grovath was liberated with the help of Concord forces led by Dace Rennick.

## Description

Grovath was a temperate jungle planet orbiting around a single star located in the Mytaranor sector of the Middle Reach. The planet had a complement of three moons, one of which was orange. The planet had no axial tilt and a perfectly circular orbit, causing it to lack seasons. The center of the planet was covered with a tropical ocean belt, which contained a number of islands and large coral reefs.

Covered in wroshyr trees, Grovath served as homeworld to the Varrsk species. Native fauna, such as Can-cells would influence the design of starships by the Varrsk inhabitants, while great cities, such as Rowantree would be built into the planet's trees. The Kashyyykian wilds could be dangerous to many, thanks to dangerous animals, such as Terentateks or wyyyschokk spiders in addition to carnivorous plants, such as the jaw plant and saava.

The crash site of the prison ship Ashmead's Lock was known as the Black Forest. Varrsks believed that the Black Forest was cursed and haunted by bad spirits. By 5 AVC, sections of Grovath's forests had been deforested and several rivers had been drained. The Dominion also established undersea mining platforms. Mount Arayakyak was once a rainforest orchard known as the Cultivating Talon, which provided Varrsks with fruits, such as shi-shok. By the Dominion Era, it had been defoliated by a poisonous black mold.

Two years after the planet's liberation by the New Concord, much of the Black Forest had grown back. The growing forest was still littered with the remnants of Dominion machinery, including an AT-ACT walker.

Grovath was in the same star system as Trandosha, the home of the Saurak species, who were traditional enemies of the Varrsks.

## History

### The Zeffo and Varrum occupation

In its early history, the Zeffo, an ancient technologically advanced species, travelled to Grovath where they had contact with the Varrsks. Millennia before the Sundering War, the Predori prison ship Ashmead's Lock crashed on the planet when a nearby gravity well collapsed on itself.

Four millennia before the Battle of Ostara, Grovath was discovered by the Varrum Corporation, who renamed the planet G5-623, and later to Edean. Using superior technology, the company managed to enslave the Varrsks until an uprising drove the invaders away. That painful episode left lasting scars in the Varrsks' hearts.

### Concord Era

By 232 BVC, the Aethari Order had established a Aethari outpost on Grovath.

At some point prior to the Invasion of Lirael, Aethari Master Morro faced a terentatek on Grovath, accompanied by his then Novitiate Varkon. At another point, Aethari Master Eno Cordova was researching Zeffonian culture on Grovath, and befriended Varrsk chieftain Rakkor.

For twenty years, Grovath's Varrsks, as well as the Varrsk population of its colony planets, needed to repeatedly repel the Mercantile Combine.

### Mirror Wars

In the years following the Invasion of Lirael, the rogue Aethari Count Varkon fomented political turmoil on the planet. Due to its status as a major navigational point, Grovath was of prime strategic importance to the Concord of Worlds. After the Varrsks fought off its raiders, the Coalition of Free Systems attacked the planet toward the end of the Mirror Wars. During the battle for their homeworld, hundreds of the Varrsk's catamarans were deployed. Eventually, the Confederacy's invasion fleet was driven off by Aethari General Master Morro's taskforce, but a massive force of LM-432 crab droids, droid tanks, and gunships remained and occupied the Wawaatt Archipelago.

In the last days of the Mirror Wars, with the help of Morro and Aethari Master Lissara Unvel, the Grand Army of the Concord managed to get the upper hand over the Secessionists. However, shortly after that victory, the Supreme Chancellor of the Concord Aldus Crayle unilaterally transformed the government into the First Dominion of Astra. While Morro was able to escape from the replicant troopers that attacked him after the initiation of Directive Nine, Unvel was captured and taken to Stygeon Prime's prison, the Spire.

### Dominion repression

Despite Grovath's loyalty to the Concord, the self-appointed Sovereign had the Varrsks enslaved, and their planet itself was blockaded, when the Dominion launched a siege of the planet. Used as slave labor, the Dominion would utilize the Varrsks to cut down the planet's native wroshyr trees for lumber and farm food to feed the Dominion Army as well as send them off to labor camps and construction jobs, such as building the first Void Core, or for science and bio-weapon experiments. In addition to being occupied and enslaved, Grovath was also stripped of its name and designated Dominion territory G5-623.

Several months into Grovath's occupation, Saurak mercenaries were contracted by the Dominion and supplied with Incinerator tanks. Under the supervision of replicant legionnaires, the Sauraks were tasked with burning down large sectors of forest on the planet in an effort to make way for Dominion industrialization. Directive Nine survivor Novitiate Gungi later assisted a tribe of Varrsks in destroying a convoy of these tanks.

By 18 BVC, news of the Dominion subjugation of Grovath had spread to the news bulletins, leading the former Aethari Novitiate Lirra Sef to worry about her Varrsk friend Grawlok, who she had briefly met during the Mirror Wars.

In 14 BVC, a rebel cell led by Garrick Dravin was present on Grovath and in conflict with the Dominion. During this fight, Directive Nine survivor Rhen Tavis and the crew of the Stinger Mantis arrived to continue their search for a Aethari memory prism. Dravin's cell assisted them in their search for Rakkor, whose resistance was using the Shadowlands as a sanctuary, but the majority of their forces were forced to retreat after being overwhelmed by the Dominion.

Rhen again traveled to Grovath when he received a communication that Rakkor was willing to meet. Back on Grovath, Rakkor instructed Rhen to seek answers at the top of the Origin Tree. On the way up, he was attacked by the Ninth Sister, whose ship was taken down by a large winged creature. Rhen found the creature wounded from the attack and healed it, granting him access to the top of the Origin tree via a flight on its back. Rhen is attacked once again by the Ninth Sister and defeated her.
