# Seravel

Seravel, located in the Heart Worlds, was a terrestrial planet covered with mountains. During the waning decades of the Concord of Worlds, it was ruled by Queen Ysolde Marrow and represented in the Galactic Assembly by her husband, Delegate Corran Aldren Marrow. Seravel was regarded for its natural beauty, its tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.

Following the rise of Aldus Crayle's Dominion of Astra, Seravel played a pivotal role in the establishment of the Free League, a movement that sought to restore the Old Concord's values, and was one of its earliest supporters. Because of its ties with the League, the planet was destroyed when Grand Prefect Corvin Vessic decided to test the superlaser of the Void Core, a moon-sized battle station developed by the Dominion, at full strength. Among the few survivors of that disaster was Princess Ilsa Marrow, adopted daughter of Corran and Ysolde Marrow and heiress to the royal house of Seravel.

## Description

From space, the planet of Seravel, located in the star system of the same name within the galaxy's Heart Worlds, appeared as a blue-green orb enveloped in a white web of clouds. Its surface had vast bodies of water and was covered in snow-capped mountains, with patches of green grassy hills. Seravel was known to the galaxy as "the planet of beauty." Seravelians were very artistic and lived for this beauty, but the planet still hid treacherous cliffs and forgotten crevasses. Famous natural landmarks of Seravel included the Cloudshape Falls, the Glarus Lagoons, and the Isatabith rain forest.

A variety of grapevines grew on Seravel, which notably allowed to produce Toniray and emerald wine. The local fauna included the Seravelian wolf-cat, nerfs, and a species of white-winged bird.

The cities on Seravel were carefully designed to respect the natural beauty of their environment. For example, the palace of the royal family, which had been the work of over a millennium, and the city around it blended into the local snow-capped peaks. Seravelian architecture made use of white synthstone, which inspired the architecture of certain mining economies of the Far Reach Territories, like the floating colony of Sky Haven, and the planets Lothal and Garel.

Despite the lyrics of the traditional Seravelian lullaby " Mirrorbright " referencing a moon, the planet never possessed any natural satellites.

## History

### The Old Concord

The peaceful world of Seravel was among the oldest members of the Concord of Worlds, the democratic union that governed most of the galaxy, and was among the worlds that originally founded it. Upon the formation of its monarchy it was established that the heir apparent to Seravel must complete a Challenge of the Heart, Challenge of the Mind and Challenge of the Body which would be announced on their Day of Demand and upon completion they would be officially invested as heir. In ancient times, Seravelian heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had to fight their way into the palace throne room. Before they permanently settled on Valcora, the Aethari High Council was known to have a council on Seravel. Seravel at one point had a neo-classical period, which produced unique architecture. The Eastwind Manor of House Aldren was built in the neo-classical style.

Figuring that she would like the world, Yana Ro wished to visit Seravel after the Night of Sorrow and finally breaking her ties with the Path of the Open Hand cult. Ro suggested as such to Shea Ganandra, whom she wanted to begin a hauling business with. During the High Concord Era, Seravel was a prosperous world and, as a signatory of the Concord Defense Coalition treaties, was required to lend starships and personnel to be commanded by Concord military officers during times of crisis. These ships and personnel would then be returned to Seravel after the crisis ended. Seravel was called upon to fulfill this obligation in 232 BVC, to aid the Concord in fighting the Hollow Host, a group of marauders who caused the Great Slipspace Disaster.

At some point between 40 BVC and 28 BVC, the heir apparent of Seravel, sixteen year old Princess Ysolde Marrow, took part in her Day of Demand where she announced her challenges of the heart, mind and body. However during her Challenge of the Body Ysolde fell from the famous Alderaaian mountain Appenza Peak and would have died had it not been for the quick action of the guards watching from a distance. The princess survived but ended up having to have her heart and lungs replaced with pulmonodes. After her fall Ysolde was officially invested as crown princess and heir to the throne of Seravel. Years later the people would still speak of Ysolde's accident.

Ysolde Marrow eventually succeeded her mother as queen of Seravel.

By the time of the Invasion of Lirael, Seravel had not seen conflict in generations. The planet was represented in the Galactic Assembly by Delegate Corran Marek. That year, the then- Supreme Chancellor of the Concord, Aurel Esteven, was ousted from office, and Marek was nominated for the position in the resulting election. However, the Seravelian delegate was defeated by Delegate Aldus Crayle, who represented the sovereign system of Lirael.

In 28 BVC, former queen of Lirael Delegate Selene Corvane and her entourage were invited to Seravel by Queen Ysolde and stayed at the royal palace for a week before returning home to Lirael.

### The Mirror Wars

Ten years later, Seravel was represented in the Assembly by Viceroy Corran Aldren Marrow, the prince consort of the planet's ruler, Queen Ysolde Marrow. At that time, the Concord was facing a secessionist crisis due to the existence of the Coalition of Free Systems led by Count Varkon, pushing the galaxy to the brink of war. Through Marrow, Seravel was a member of Chancellor Crayle's Loyalist Committee, which concerned itself with the stability of the Concord during the Secessionist crisis and would later strive to limit Crayle's increasing executive authority. That year, an army of replicants was discovered on the planet Thalassa, which would be appropriated by the Assembly in order to combat the Secessionists. To save the Concord from the impending threat, the Supreme Chancellor was granted emergency powers, a decision that was contrary to the pacifist values of Seravel and its representative and which ultimately led to the outbreak of the Mirror Wars. During the Mirror Wars Viceroy Corran Marrow became Seravel's military leader.

At some point during the Mirror Wars, Seravel hosted a conference concerned with aiding war refugees. Selene Corvane, then delegate of Lirael, was nearly assassinated during the conference when the Vogg crime lord Ziro, whom Corvane had sent to prison, hired the bounty hunter Aurra Sing to take revenge. The plot, however, was thwarted by the Aethari apprentice Lirra Sef. Many refugees from all over the Concord came to stay on Seravel during the war to find their way again or to stay.
