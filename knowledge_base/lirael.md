# Lirael

Lirael was a bountiful planet in the Chommell sector of the Middle Reach, in the Trailing Sectors and close to the border of the Far Reach Territories. It was home to the indigenous Pellan species and to a population of humans known as the Lirael. Lirael was pushed to the forefront of galactic politics as the birthplace of the Dark Lord of the Morrak Aldus Crayle, who served as its representative in the Assembly of the Concord of Worlds.

Under the guise of his Morrak title Xarn Nocturne, Crayle secretly engineered an invasion of his peaceful homeworld by the Mercantile Combine, a megacorporation in a bid for power. Ostensibly done in protest over proposed taxation of formerly free trade routes, the Federation's invasion led to an outpouring of support from the Assembly for both Crayle and the newly-elected Queen of Lirael, Selene Corvane. Corvane led a successful resistance movement and removed the Federation from her world during the Battle of Lirael. Crayle subsequently rose to the office of the Supreme Chancellor of the Concord, with Corvane eventually taking his place as the planet's senatorial representative.

Lirael would commit itself firmly to democracy in the intervening years, staying loyal to the Concord during the pan-galactic Mirror Wars, which saw several military engagements against the Coalition of Free Systems come to the planet. By the time of the war's end, Crayle had amassed considerable power and proclaimed himself the head of a new Dominion of Astra. Crayle's reign would eventually be ended by the League to Restore the Concord, a rebel movement led by Corvane's children, Ilsa Marrow and Tavin Vossar.

Despite his origins on the planet, Crayle did not care at all for his homeworld and, following his death, Lirael was named a priority target for Operation: Cinder, a protocol that the Morrak Lord had put in place to destroy Dominion worlds should they fail to protect their Sovereign. Through the intervention of Marrow and Queen Sosha Soruna, however, Lirael was spared from ruin and went on to become a member of the successor state to the League, the New Concord. Decades later, the planet would again fight against tyranny, rising up against the military junta of the Iron Mandate during its war with Marrow's Ember Front.

## Description

Lirael was a small pastoral world in the Middle Reach, located near the border of the Far Reach Territories, 34,000 light-years from the Heart Worlds. Unlike most other worlds, the ancient planet lacked a molten core, and instead comprised a conglomerate of large, rocky bodies that made up a network of tunnels and flooded caves. The native Pellans, who built their homes in the waters of Lirael, so deep that they went right through the planet, which they rarely ventured into the core, fearing the ravenous sea beasts which resided in areas such as the Caves of Eleuabad. Despite this, certain Pellan navigators utilized time-honored trade routes through the planet's structure, which served as the most expedient avenues to reach other areas of the planet. Lirael's interior was rich with plasma, a naturally-occurring energy unique to the planet, and this coupled with its lack of molten core gave Lirael a reputation as an enigma to astrophysicists, who regarded the world's structure as an extremely rare phenomenon in the galaxy.

Lirael's surface comprised a vast array of different landscapes, from rolling plains and grassy hills to swampy lakes caused by the water-filled network of deep-sea tunnels. The swamps acted as a gateway to the world's seas, where legendary creatures dwelt. Beside its natural features, Lirael was considered a world of classical beauty due to the aesthetics of its population centers. The porous crust's natural plasma was harvested for energy and building material, and was generally thought to be the key to many of the planet's secrets.

While most of Lirael's land animals were peaceful, as exemplified by the gentle shaak, the planet's oceans, which held an abundance of life, teemed with menacing creatures, such as the opee sea killer, the sando aqua monster, and the colo claw fish.

## History

Originally, Lirael was home to the amphibian Pellans. The earliest offworlders to arrive were a Reptilian species known as the Elders who warred against the Pellans. Eventually, the Elders vanished as mysteriously as they arrived, leaving only stone statues behind.

Approximately around 4000 BVC, Grizmallti people had discovered the planet, which they had named Nabu, after one of their principal deities. Lirael quickly became notorious as a vacation spot and as a private hunting preserve amongst big-game hunters across the Middle Reach, although it would be around a century before permanent human settlement. Initially, the Pellans retreated underwater and avoided the colonists and new interlopers.

While the Pellans flourished for thousands of years before, this only lasted until around 3900 BVC. Fleeing a brutal civil war, a group of humans from the planet Grizmallt crash-landed on Lirael and set up a small colony. As they established their own society, they initially kept separate from the Pellans. Led by Kwilaan, the colonists ventured into the Gallo Mountains, created a farming community at the Dee'ja Peak, establishing their presence on the planet and eventually taking its name for themselves. The Lirael calendar based its epoch around the arrival of Kwilaan. When the colonists made contact with the Pellans, tensions arose between the two peoples, culminating a series of wars between the two species. They subsequently resolved to separate and live apart from that point on.

The Lirael colonists later expanded to other uninhabited planets in the Chommell sector, such as Karlinus. Lirael imposed a contract on the colonists of these other planets, wherein Lirael would give the new colonies a loan to cover the start-up costs of colonization. In exchange, the colonies became legally obligated to provide Lirael with any supplies it deemed were necessary if called upon in times of emergency. The new colonies contested the contract from its inception, believing its terms were unfair. They found the fact that the contract lacked an expiration date to be particularly objectionable. Under the contract, Lirael took advantage of every other planet in the Chommell sector from time to time.

Following the wars with humans, the Pellan tribes engaged in war with each other. The war came to an end due to the actions of Boss Gallo, a Pellan who united the tribes under one banner through diplomatic and military means. Gallo eventually attacked the city Spearhead, which was the home of the rogue warlord Boss Rogoe and the other Pellan holdouts, with a massive Pellan army. Gallo then founded the Pellan capital Otoh Gunga on Spearhead's foundations.

Meanwhile, the Lirael had fallen prey to a long period of brutal infighting among the large city-states of humans that inhabited the planet. Eventually, a general named Jafan managed to end the war, uniting the opposing factions through both force of arms and force of will. He became King Jafan, ruler of all the Lirael, and ushered in the Great Time of Peace.
