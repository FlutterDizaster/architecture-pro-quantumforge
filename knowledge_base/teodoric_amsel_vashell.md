# Teodoric Amsel Vashell

Teodoric Amsel Vashell, otherwise known as Teodor Vashell, was a human smuggler, gambler, and entrepreneur who became Baron Administrator of Sky Haven and, later, a general in the Free League. Born on Socorro, he was the owner of the Crimson Kestrel before losing it to Dace Rennick in a game of tarrok on Numidian Prime. Afterward, Vashell put an end to his days as a smuggler and became an entrepreneur, setting up a small mining operation on the planet Lothal before eventually becoming the leader of Sky Haven in the skies of the planet Nimbara.

During the Sundering War, Xarn Velgor arrived on Sky Haven to lay a trap for his son, Tavin Vossar. As part of the trap, the Dark Lord of the Morrak forced Vashell into tricking a group of Insurgents, including Rennick and Princess Ilsa Marrow, leading them to Velgor himself. Though Velgor promised to leave Sky Haven without an Dominion presence, Vashell felt the deal had been altered to the point where he could no longer tolerate it. Vashell alerted his citizens to the Dominion of Astra's presence and impending occupation and ordered an evacuation. He helped the Insurgents try to rescue Rennick, who had been frozen in stasium, from Jask Morvain, but the bounty hunter escaped.

Vashell joined the Free League and set out to find Rennick. After locating him in the palace of Hrask the Vogg on Drysa, Vashell aided in his rescue. The Insurgents returned to the fleet, and Vashell became a general, volunteering to lead the assault on the VC-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station during the Battle of Sylvar. During the battle, he piloted the Crimson Kestrel into the battle station's core, firing the shot which destroyed it.

In the years of peace after the Dominion's dissolution, Vashell started a family and fathered a daughter named Kadara Vashell. However, while she was only an infant, his daughter was kidnapped by the Iron Mandate. Six years later, he embarked on a quest to the desert planet of Dustfall with Tavin Vossar to find answers about the growing darkness in the Flux, but failed to uncover anything. Seeking to bury the pain of those memories, Vashell settled on Dustfall and led a solitary lifestyle where he was known as the " Hermit " to the Aki-Aki of the Lurch Canyon. He lived alone for some years while keeping contact with the Rennick family and was later asked by Marrow's Ember Front for his aid in the war against the Iron Mandate.

In 35 AVC, Teodor helped the Ember Front and reunited with his old friend, Grawlok. Vashell played one more role in the fight for galactic freedom as he brought an assembled Citizens' Fleet to reinforce the Ember Front at the battle at Umbrath, from which the Ember Front emerged victorious.

## Biography

### Deals and adventures

A member of the tragic Vashell family, Teodoric Amsel Vashell, otherwise known as "Teodor" Vashell, was born on the planet Socorro. During his youth, he became a smuggler and a gambler, excelling in a card game known as tarrok. He traveled throughout the galaxy and piloted a freighter called the Crimson Kestrel, which he won through a game of tarrok at the gas giant Nimbara. The ship saved his life on numerous occasions. Sometime before 10 BVC, Teodor won a subtropical moon in the Oseon Belt during a game of tarrok. Teodor also developed a partnership with M4-12, a custom self-made piloting droid.

During his time with M4, Teodor nearly lost her to a Vogg after making a bad bet in a game of tarrok on Gonda. At one point, Vashell was involved in an incident with a Dawinian and their companion. He also came to meet the Tonnika sisters, even showing Brea Tonnika how to work the controls on the Kestrel. At another point, Brea and Senni Tonnika made a mess of the room they were staying in on the city-world of Valcora in order to get back at Vashell. At some point, Vashell met a droid mechanic who worked on the Middle Reach planet Kijimi, with that mechanic coming to owe Vashell a favor.

Sometime prior to the Battle of Ostara, Teodor and M4-12 were ambushed in the Halthor sector when an Dominion interdictor dropped out from slipspace. As a result of the ambush, Vashell lost a majority of his shipment of walkers that he acquired from an Er'Kit arms dealer in the Noonian sector. Teodor also had dealings with Scarlet Veil on Felucia. He completed his debt to Scarlet Veil and its leader, Dryden Vos. At another point, Vashell visited Pantora and successfully convinced Dominion Vice Grand Administrator Prita Sven to let him leave the planet with a freighter full of poached forlyn carcasses.

Vashell and M4-12 at some point was arrested for smuggling and was sent to the planet Hynestia. Teodor and M4 was asked by Queen Forsythia Jin of Hynestia to deliver an artifact to the Dominion of Astra for making up for a gherlian fur failed delivery. Princess Rinetta Gan knew about the real purpose of the artifact from her teacher Zel Gris. It was supposed to function as an energy source and make up for the lack of sunlight on the planet Livno III. Without it, Livno III would fall to ruin and destruction. Rinetta tried to force Vashell and M4 to comply with her new orders after sneaking onto the Crimson Kestrel as a stowaway. Her plan failed as Vashell traveled to Neral's moon to settle a debt with Ne'eda Frip. When the exchange went wrong and escaped but he was captured by Queen Forsythia again. Queen Forsythia put Vashell and M4 in jail. Rinetta freed Vashell and M4 again. She wanted them to help her return the artifact to Livno III. They all escaped Hynestia and had to defeat Ne'eda's starships before making the jump to Livno III. After returning the artifact to Livno III, they put the princess Rinetta back to Hynestia. Queen Forsythia pardoned Vashell and M4 afterwards.

Vashell later smuggled weapons to Petrusia after the Dominion seized control of it. Following that operation, Vashell considered turning the Kestrel into a casino, despite M4's reservations about it. He also started to write the Vashell Chronicles to show the galaxy his prodigiousness. Vashell and M4 took the Kestrel to Batuv where he tried to by a new cape from Borkus until he met the bartender Kristiss. She told Vashell of her people's plight and status as slaves at the Dominion outpost on Kullgroon. Kristiss asked for his help in arming her people for 1,000 credits up front. Despite his reservations, Vashell decided to speak with her and have a drink with her.
