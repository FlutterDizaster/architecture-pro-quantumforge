# Selene Corvane Talvane

Selene Corvane Talvane, also simply known as Selene Corvane, was a human female politician who represented the people of Lirael during the final years of the Concord of Worlds. Prior to her career as a delegate in the Galactic Assembly, Corvane was the elected ruler of the Royal House of Lirael. A political idealist, she advocated for the preservation of democracy as well as a peaceful resolution to the Mirror Wars. However, her secret marriage to the Aethari Knight Kaelen Vossar would have a lasting effect on the future of the galaxy for decades to come.

Born in 46 BVC during the Age of the Concord, Selene Talvane was raised by her parents, Loran Talvane and his wife Jobal, on the peaceful Middle Reach world of Lirael. Despite the humble background of the Talvane family, Selene was identified early for her potential in public service. Devoting her life to civic duty, she was elected queen and, therefore, adopted the regnal name "Corvane" in 32 BVC. Due to the machinations of her advisor, Delegate Aldus Crayle, Corvane's reign was put to the test by the Mercantile Combine's blockade and subsequent invasion of Lirael. Unable to fight the Federation's army of battle droids without assistance, the queen beseeched the Galactic Assembly on Valcora for help, only to see her efforts hampered by bureaucracy and procedure. Although she ultimately liberated her homeworld with the help of the Aethari Order and the Pellan Grand Army, her decision to cast a Vote of No Confidence against Aurel Esteven enabled Crayle to become the Supreme Chancellor.

Following her reign, Corvane joined the Assembly and struggled to resolve the Secessionist Crisis through diplomacy, becoming a leading voice in the Loyalist Committee by 22 BVC. After surviving an assassination attempt planned by the Mercantile Combine, she was removed from the capital and sent back to Lirael under the protection of Novitiate Kaelen Vossar. Though the Aethari Code forbade personal relationships, deeming attachment, and possession unbecoming of a Aethari, the two gradually became closer as their feelings for each other grew. In the aftermath of the First Battle of Karrik, Corvane secretly married her Aethari protector, despite her own reservations about their relationship and the consequences they would face if the truth ever came to light. Their subsequent interactions were limited throughout the Mirror Wars, with Corvane continuing to advocate peaceful solutions in the Assembly while Vossar served on the front lines as a Aethari General in the Grand Army of the Concord.

In the waning months of the pan-galactic conflict, Corvane revealed to her husband that she was pregnant. As a result of Vossar's fear of loss, he was seduced by the dark side of the Flux, believing that by becoming a Morrak Lord he would gain the power to prevent his wife from dying in childbirth. Corvane witnessed Sovereign Crayle's declaration that her fears had come true; the Concord had become the Dominion of Astra. After she learned of her husband's transformation into a Morrak, she confronted him on Pyrrhos in the hopes that he had not truly changed, but he instead attempted to kill her. She ultimately died of a broken heart after giving birth to the twins Tavin Vossar and Ilsa Corvane Vossar. With her dying breath, she insisted that there was still good in the former Kaelen Vossar, who embraced the persona of Xarn Velgor throughout the reign of the Dominion.

Corvane's children played a major role in the fragmentation of the Dominion and the redemption of her husband. Like her mother, Ilsa entered public service and became a flag-bearer for the democratic ideals of liberty and justice as a leader in the League to Restore the Concord and its successor, the New Concord, before becoming the founding general of the Ember Front against the Iron Mandate —a successor state to the Dominion of Astra. Tavin, on the other hand, was trained in the ways of the Aethari like his father before him and was a celebrated war hero of the Free League. Through Tavin's efforts to redeem his father back to the light, Corvane's last words proved true when Kaelen killed the Sovereign during the Battle of Sylvar.

## Biography

### Early life

A human female of the Talvane family, the young woman best remembered as Selene Corvane was born Selene Talvane on the bountiful planet of Lirael in 46 BVC. She was the younger daughter of Loran and Jobal Talvane and the sister of Iriel Talvane. Her father was a friend of Delegate Onaconda Farr, who represented the solar system of Rodia in the Assembly of the Concord of Worlds. Talvane was very fond of the Rodian, whom she referred to as "Uncle Ono."

As a child, she baked every day and traveled with her father on relief missions to offer aid to suffering planets. When Talvane was seven, she traveled with her father and Farr to Shadda-Bi-Boran to help relocate the population in the wake of an ecological disaster. Unfortunately, the effort resulted in failure, as the Shadda-Bi-Boran could not adapt to their new homeworld. It led her to declare later in life that she would try 10,000 times in order to save one person.

### Rise to queenship

Although her parents were of modest origins, Talvane was identified as a gifted and brilliant young girl at an early age. She devoted herself to public service, joining the Apprentice Legislature at the tender age of eight, and she eventually became a junior senatorial adviser. She attended the Elmira Royal Academy to learn how to become a political leader.

At age twelve, Talvane participated in the Legislative Youth Program, in which she met a slightly older boy named Palo Jemabie. The two fell in love, but their relationship did not last, as they parted ways when Jemabie left politics and chose to become an artist. At the same age, she started a two- year mandate as supervisor of Elmira, the capital city of Lirael.

Two years later, Selene Talvane began her campaign to become the next Queen of Lirael. She campaigned herself as "Candidate Corvane," with anonymity being customary for candidates and monarchs. She portrayed herself as the calm and composed candidate. Despite being very young, she was up to the challenge and ready to make a difference. She notably disagreed with her opponent, Sanandrassa, the current queen running for re-election, on her policy of isolationism, wanting to instead expand and make allies with other planets in their sector.

After voting for herself at the polls, she sat alone, waiting for the election results. Captain and Head of Security Quarsh Panaka walked in and tell her that she had won the election and her work had begun. Meanwhile, Governor Sio Bibble announced to the planet's population that Candidate Corvane had won. She adopted Corvane as her regnal name. Jemabie created a poster entitled Lirael Coronation to publicize her coronation as Monarch of Lirael. Although she was not the youngest sovereign ever elected, it had been a long time since a Lirael queen had been as young as her, and she would later question her own maturity at the time of her election.
