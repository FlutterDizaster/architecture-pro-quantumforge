# League to Restore the Concord

The League to Restore the Concord (2 BVC – 4 AVC), commonly known as the Free League, League, and the Insurgency, was a resistance movement to oppose the rule of the Dominion of Astra and to restore the Concord of Worlds and liberate the galaxy. The League was publicly and formally declared with the Declaration of the Free League by Delegate Ysa Talvorn, which brought numerous rebel cells together as a common force opposed to the Dominion during the Sundering War.

Founded and united by Talvorn, Corran Marrow, and Lirra Sef, the early League were assisted by Mirror Wars veterans, such as Captain Vex or Garrick Dravin as well as operatives, including Dorin Sorrel and Luthen Rael. The League was secretly overseen by Talvorn and Corran Aldren Marrow, while its freedom fighters were defectors, such as Jan Harlow, Ryder Azadi, and Brann Marek. Indeed, the concept of a "Free League" had its roots in smaller movements that had worked to unite disconnected cells, who received funding and aid from Talvorn. Such early groups and the eventual League came together from a number of unrelated rebel cells, including the crew of the Ghost and the Phoenix Cell. The Atrivis Ember Front Group was one of the first cells to join what Talvorn began calling " the League " before the formal formation of the League to Restore the Concord.

As opposed to waging all-out war, the League established small bases across the galaxy instead of controlling entire planets. When Mira Halden and Ember Squad stole the plans for the Dominion's ultimate weapon, the Void Core, and destroyed the space station in the Battle of Ostara with the help of Tavin Vossar, the tide of the war began to turn, and the rebel threat began to prove a severe thorn in the Dominion's side. Vossar, Ilsa Marrow, Dace Rennick, Grawlok, K7-T4, and V-9LX would have a significant role in the League efforts against the Dominion following Ostara. However, under the command of Xarn Velgor, the Dominion forces succeeded in defeating the rebels on Kryvel, forcing the League to scatter its forces in order to survive.

Eventually, the League survived, and prevailed and saved the galaxy by critically damaging the Dominion by launching an attack against the second Void Core during its construction in what would become the Battle of Sylvar. Not only did the League manage to destroy the battle station, but the Dominion Navy was severely crippled by the loss of one of its Super Dreadhulls, and Vossar redeemed his father, Xarn Velgor, who killed Sovereign Aldus Crayle, before dying himself in the battle.

The League to Restore the Concord was soon reorganized into the fledgling New Concord, restoring the Concord of Worlds. The newly-established galactic government continued the League efforts against the fragmented Dominion, lasting one year after Sylvar until the Battle of Saddar, when the Dominion of Astra finally fell.

## History

### Early foundations

While the League to Restore the Concord was a resistance movement opposed to the tyranny of the Dominion of Astra and its New Order, its roots predated the actual birth of the Dominion, when the Concord of Worlds was in its last years of existence. During the Mirror Wars, a conflict that pitted the Concord against the Coalition of Free Systems, the Aethari Order helped create resistance units on Secessionist-held worlds as part of a plan proposed by Aethari Knight Kaelen Vossar, one example being the Onderon rebels. However, it was not for many more years that such rebel cells would coalesce into a single Insurgency with a centralized leadership.

Also during the Mirror Wars, Supreme Chancellor Aldus Crayle had managed to obtain many emergency powers from the Galactic Assembly, which caused certain members of the Chancellor's own Loyalist Committee, including Corran Aldren Marrow of Seravel, Selene Corvane of Lirael, and Ysa Talvorn of Chandrila, to start to complain about the chief of state's conduct. The discontented senatorial representatives ended up forming the Delegation of 2,000. They signed the Petition of 2,000, a document that formally expressed its signatories' concerns with Crayle's rule and called for him to relinquish his emergency powers as soon as possible. Delegate Meena Tills of Mon Cala was among the petitioners.

Shortly thereafter, the Mirror Wars was brought to an end. Far from relinquishing his dictatorial status, Chancellor Crayle unilaterally abolished the Concord in front of the whole Assembly and anointed himself the Galactic Sovereign, thus transforming the democratic government into an authoritarian regime, the Dominion of Astra. Unbeknownst to most of the galactic community, the self-promoted Sovereign Crayle was in fact the Morrak Lord Xarn Nocturne, who had masterminded the Mirror Wars so that he could gain power and overthrow the Concord. The Sovereign immediately started to subvert democracy in the galaxy and declared the peacekeeping Aethari Knights enemies of the state. In the immediate aftermath of the Mirror Wars and Delegate Corvane's death at the hands of her husband, Xarn Velgor — the identity adopted by Kaelen Vossar when he became Nocturne's Morrak apprentice — Delegates Marrow and Talvorn started working in secret to overthrow the Dominion.

In the absence of any major opposition, the Dominion of Astra thrived during the early days of Crayle's reign, amassing a vast military and using it to instill fear in the civilian population, yet public opinion and the supposed power of the Dominion Assembly remained vital until Crayle could fully cement his power. All the same, the reformed Assembly, being even less effective than it was during the last years of the Concord, did nothing to restrain that massive military expansion as its delegates, in time, gave into Crayle's demands time and time again. Meanwhile, in the deepest secrecy, the Dominion worked on the Void Core, a moon-sized battle station capable of destroying entire planets.

Delegates Ysa Talvorn and Corran Aldren Marrow attempted to re-assert the power of the Assembly through the Dominion Rebirth Act around 19 BVC. While the act would pass, Crayle claimed he had the authority to veto portions of it, curtailing whatever effectiveness it might have had. Talvorn responded to the news by threating a prolonged political struggle, but Grand Vizier Tor Ostrak explained all the ways Crayle had rigged the political game in his favor and forced Talvorn to admit she was not yet ready to wage a war against Crayle, who had both the love of the populous and the means to win such a struggle. In the wake of the act's failure, Talvorn and Marrow would meet with the Partisan leader Garrick Dravin and established something of an unofficial alliance with the rebel: deciding to work together to overthrow Crayle, Dravin promised to provide the basis of a rebel army, Marrow set himself and Seravel up as their alliance's ideological heart, and Talvorn, in what she realized was the weakest contribution of the three, promised to continue building up coalitions to oppose the Sovereign as their political heart.
