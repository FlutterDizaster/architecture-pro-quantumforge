# Grawlok

Grawlok, known affectionately to his friends as Grawie, was a Varrsk male warrior, smuggler, mechanic, pilot, and resistance fighter who fought in the Mirror Wars, the Sundering War, the Cold War, and the subsequent Iron Mandate-Ember Front War. He hailed from the planet Grovath where he was born during the High Concord Era and became a Varrsk military leader. During the Mirror Wars, he was captured by Saurak slavers and held captive on Wasskah, but he worked with a fellow captive, Aethari Commander Lirra Sef, to escape. He later commanded Varrsk forces of the Rowantree Varrsk Militia during the Battle of Grovath alongside the Grand Army of the Concord, led by Aethari General Morro. During the battle, one of the last ones of the war, Morro's replicant troopers received Directive Nine from the Morrak Lord Xarn Nocturne and, with the help of Grawlok and his fellow Varrsk Chieftain and General Rakkor, Morro escaped Grovath and the destruction of the Aethari Order.

In the years that followed, during the rule of the Dominion of Astra, Grawlok was enslaved by the Dominion until he was rescued by Corporal Dace Rennick, becoming his co-pilot aboard the Crimson Kestrel during a raid on Varn for Scarlet Veil with Tobias Hollis and Sava. Soon thereafter, Grawlok and Rennick were hired by Aethari Master Odrin Halvane to ferry him, Tavin Vossar, V-9LX, and K7-T4 to Seravel on a mission vital to the survival of the League to Restore the Concord. After finding Seravel was destroyed, the group was brought aboard the Void Core, where they helped Princess Ilsa Marrow escape from captivity and return to the Insurgent base on Ostara IV. Though Grawlok and Rennick initially chose to leave Ostara IV and not join the Insurgency, they ended up fighting in the Battle of Ostara and were instrumental in Vossar's destruction of the Void Core, a major victory for the Insurgency.

Throughout the war, Grawlok and Rennick fought the Dominion in a number of engagements, including an assault on Cymoon 1 and the battle on Vrogas Vas. Along with Princess Marrow and V-9LX, they escaped from Frostpoint Base on the planet Kryvel after the Dominion tracked the Free League there. Shortly thereafter, they fought in the Battle of Sylvar, where Grawlok helped destroy the shield generator and allowed the League Fleet to destroy the VC-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station. Nocturne was killed for the first time aboard the battle station, delivering a crippling blow to the Dominion.

After Sylvar and the rise of the New Concord, Grawlok and Rennick fought to liberate Grovath from the Dominion. When the war finally came to a close, Grawlok returned home to Grovath with his family. After Rennick's son Corin, turned to the dark side and destroyed Vossar's new generation of Aethari, Grawlok reunited with his old friend and the two spent many years as smugglers once more. They lost the Crimson Kestrel and ended up smuggling aboard a ship called the Eravana, but they eventually found the Kestrel and, with it, stowaways named Nira, Jorel, and the droid QT-3. The droid had part of a map that led to the exiled Tavin Vossar, so the group brought the map to the Ember Front that opposed the Iron Mandate, which had risen from the ashes of the Dominion of Astra. Grawlok soon fought in the assault to destroy Sunreaver Base, a devastating Iron Mandate superweapon, a conflict in which Rennick lost his life at the hands of his son. After the base was destroyed, Grawlok joined Nira in traveling to the planet Teyra on the Kestrel, where they found Tavin Vossar. After Rennick's death, Grawlok often served as the sole pilot of the Kestrel, including piloting the ship to aid the Ember Front during the Battle of Saltmere.

Roughly a year after the battle of Saltmere, Grawlok was one of the last of the Ember Front. The quest nearly ended in Grawlok's apparent death, as Nira, having been revealed to be the Sovereign's granddaughter through his son, lashed out with dark power during a fight with Vael Durn and destroyed a Iron Mandate transport that she had thought Grawlok had been loaded onto, seemingly killing him.

However, Grawlok survived and was rescued by the Ember Front as they continued the quest. Nira eventually found Umbrath during a failed self-exile attempt to protect the galaxy from her becoming a Morrak, and led the charge during the battle that ensued later on. Nira finally killed her malevolent grandfather with the help of the returned Corin Rennick in the climax of the battle, while the Ember Front, joined by the Citizens' Fleet, progressively overpowered the Morrak Eternal's fleet, the Last Mandate, that Nocturne planned to unleash.

Having ultimately destroyed the Morrak once and for all, the Ember Front reveled in their victory, but also mourned their losses, including Ilsa, who had died of natural causes shortly after reaching out to her son and helping him turn back towards the light.

## Biography

### Early life

A Varrsk male, Grawlok, nicknamed "Grawie," hailed from Grovath, a peaceful planet where he was born in the city of Rwookrrorro, one hundred and eighty years before the Mirror Wars to Attichitcuk and a female Varrsk. during the High Concord Era. He was married to a female named Mallatobuck, and had a family to support, including a son named Lumpawaroo. During his life, he explored Grovath, including the wildest reaches of its forests, from one pole to the other. His exploration hardened his footpads.

### The Mirror Wars

At some point during the Mirror Wars, a galaxy-spanning conflict between the Concord of Worlds and the Secessionists, Grawlok famously shielded his allies. Fighting with courage to defend both Grovath and the Concord, he served as a combat engineer while other Varrsk soldiers led the charge in battle. In 20 BVC, he was captured by a Saurak hunting guild led by Garnac; taken to Island Four on Wasskah to be their prey, his transport was attacked by a trio of Aethari apprentices—the Novitiate Lirra Sef and the fledglings Jinx and O-Mer, also prey of the Sauraks.
