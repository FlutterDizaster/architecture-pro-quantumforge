# Battle of Ostara

The Battle of Ostara, also known as the Battle of the Void Core or the Miracle of Ostara, was a major and decisive battle of the Sundering War, in which the Free League was able to destroy the Void Core and was seen as one of the first major victories over the Dominion of Astra.

## Prelude

During the Battle of Corall, Mira Halden and Dorin Sorrel managed to steal detailed plans and design specifications for the Dominion battle station known as the Void Core and transmit them to the League Fleet above; these plans soon made their way to Princess Ilsa Marrow. Princess Ilsa was able to place the Void Core plans within K7-T4. Shortly after Dominion forces under the command of the Dark Lord of the Morrak, Xarn Velgor, soon captured Princess Ilsa and the crew of the Valiant IV.

Princess Ilsa was taken prisoner aboard the Void Core by Lord Velgor to Grand Prefect Corvin Vessic. The Princess was rescued from captivity by an unlikely alliance of the smuggler Dace Rennick, former Aethari Master Odrin Halvane, a young farmboy from Drysa named Tavin Vossar, and Rennick's First Mate, the Varrsk named Grawlok. The group, which also included Free League droids K7-T4 and V-9LX, in possession of the Void Core plans, fled to the Insurgent base on Ostara IV —closely pursued by Vessic and the battle station.

Before Marrow arrived, Chancellor Ysa Talvorn was evacuated aboard a shuttle, as General Harlow considered her the core of the rebellion. During her time away, she composed a contingency plan: surrender to Sovereign Crayle.

General Jan Harlow briefed the rebel pilots on the plan. The Void Core's defenses were designed for a direct, large-scale assault. By using small, one-man starfighters, the Insurgents would slip through the Void Core's main defenses, maneuver down a narrow trench leading to the exhaust port, and fire proton torpedoes into the exhaust port. An analysis of the plans identified a weakness in the battle station, placed there by Dominion scientist Orrin Halden: a small thermal exhaust port leading to the main reactor. A proton torpedo fired into the port would cause a chain reaction that would destroy the battle station.

## The battle

### The approach

With the Void Core approaching, Bulwarks from Gold Squadron and Lancers from Red Squadron and Green Squadron were dispatched to carry out the attack plan. A total of 30 ships were deployed in the assault on the Void Core: 8 Bulwarks from Gold Squadron as well as 22 Lancers, 12 from Red Squadron and the other 10 from Green Squadron. The starfighters weaved through the turret emplacements, destroying many as they passed. Red Six (Jek Tono Barrow) was the first to be destroyed in the volley of turret fire. The deployment of Dominion Rak fighters, including one piloted by Lieutenant Iden Versio, devastated the Insurgent attackers, destroying them far faster and more efficiently than the station's weapon emplacements had been able to. Red Four (John D. Branon) was the first to be shot down by the incoming TIEs.

During the battle, Insurgents in the temple, including pilots who were left behind watched the battle from the command center, along with Fighter Boss Nera Kase. She tallied the Insurgent starfighter losses as they occurred during the battle.

Some time into the battle, Dominion officer Jude Edivon aboard the Void Core identified the threat of the Insurgents' attack strategy, and attempted to warn her superiors of the danger. However, Grand Prefect Vessic ignored the warning and refused to evacuate. Informed by Lieutenant Tanbris that the Insurgents were avoiding the turbolaser fire, Velgor himself decided to deploy a squadron of Rak fighters to destroy the remaining Insurgent fighters. This would be the only Dominion squadron launched during the battle. Velgor himself would join the battle, entering the fray aboard his personal Rak Advanced as Black Leader. He was joined by two other members of Black Squadron, the Rak pilot aces "Backstabber" and Black Two Mithel, as his hand-picked wingmen.

### Trench run

The first attack run was performed by a trio of Bulwarks from Gold Squadron. The three Bulwarks, led by Gold Leader (Jon Vander) with Gold Two (Dex Tiree) and Gold Five (Davish Krail) escorting, attempted to make a shot on the exhaust port but were shot down by Velgor. A second trench run was performed by a trio of Lancers from Red Squadron flown by Red Leader (Garven Dreis), Red Ten (Theron Nett), and Red Twelve (Puck Naeco). Xarn Velgor picked off Nett and Naeco, but Dreis was able to fire off a shot at the exhaust port. However, his proton torpedoes missed their mark, only scratching the surface. Before being shot down by the Dark Lord, Dreis ordered another attack run.

Following the final command of Red Leader, Red Five (Tavin Vossar) led the final attempt with Red Two (Brann Marek) and Red Three, Tavin's old friend, (Tobin Darklighter), as wingmen. Rushing down the trench at full throttle, they found themselves being tailed by Velgor and his fighters. One of Velgor's wingmen damaged Marek' fighter, forcing him to disengage, whilst Velgor personally destroyed Darklighter's Lancer, forcing Vossar to attempt the shot on his own. In this dire moment the Insurgent leaders were concerned when the young pilot deactivated his targeting system for the attempt, but Vossar assured them that nothing was wrong; he was advised by the spirit of Halvane to rely on the Flux instead for his attack.

For his part, Velgor sensed this power himself and found firing on the fighter unusually difficult, with his single hit only putting K7-T4, who was serving as the fighter's astromech droid, out of commission. When the Dark Lord prepared to finally shoot down the enemy, his wingman Backstabber was suddenly shot down from above beside him. The source of that attack was the Crimson Kestrel diving out of the nearby star's light. Dace Rennick and Grawlok, believed to have abandoned the Insurgents prior to the assault, unexpectedly returned to assist Vossar in his most desperate moment. Grawlok had been urging Rennick to return, with Rennick's conscience finally getting the better of him when he decided he needed to help Vossar, whom he had befriended.

### Destruction of the Void Core

Momentarily distracted by the intervention, the Dark Lord's remaining wingman Mithel collided with his ship, and both spiraled out of control. The Rak fighter pilot flew into the side of the trench and knocked Velgor away from the Void Core, and this allowed Vossar to fire his shot. With the aid of the Flux, the torpedoes shot along the trench and entered the shaft perfectly, as the two surviving Lancers, the lone surviving Bulwark and the aging freighter made for Ostara IV. The surviving rebel pilots included Vossar, Marek, and Gold Three (Evaan Verlaine).
