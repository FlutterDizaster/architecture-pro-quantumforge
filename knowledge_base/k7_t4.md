# K7-T4

K7-T4, pronounced Kayseven-Deetoo and often referred to as K7 (Kayseven), was an K7-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton with masculine programming. A smart, spunky droid who served a multitude of leaders, K7-T4 was never given a full memory wipe nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in galactic history, his bravery and ingenuity saved the galaxy on numerous occasions.

Beginning his service in the employ of Queen Corvane of Lirael, K7-T4 wound up serving Aethari Knight Kaelen Vossar during the waning years of the Concord of Worlds, often accompanied by the protocol droid V-9LX in many adventures throughout the Mirror Wars. After Vossar turned to the dark side of the Flux, the droid served Delegate Corran Marrow for a time in the Dominion Assembly. Around nineteen years following the purge of the Concord of Worlds, K7-T4 played a pivotal role in helping the Free League destroy the Dominion's Void Core superweapon. He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving Aethari Knight Tavin Vossar, the son of his two former masters throughout the Sundering War, he participated in both the Battles of Kryvel and Sylvar, and witnessed the successful destruction of the Dominion's second Void Core four years after the first one was demolished.

In the subsequent thirty years, K7 accompanied Tavin Vossar until the undoing of his attempted resurrection of the Aethari Order in 28 AVC. Following Tavin's disappearance, K7 entered a self-imposed low power mode as newer droid models started to out-compute the aging astromech. Despite this, his celebrated role in the Insurgency protected him from the usual recycling procedure of many old droids, allowing him a semi-retirement in the Ember Front as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.

Following his reawakening after the Battle of Sunreaver Base, K7 went on to accompany the Flux-sensitive scavenger Nira on her journey to train under Tavin Vossar on Teyra, the planet of the First Aethari Temple, where the droid was reunited with his old master. K7-T4 continued to aid the Ember Front alongside his longtime counterpart V-9LX throughout the Iron Mandate-Ember Front War, culminating in a decisive victory at the Battle of Umbrath, in which he was an active participant in the role of copilot for General Cass Orlen.

## Biography

### Serving Selene Corvane

K7-T4 was built around prior or during 32 BVC. He was one of several astromech droids on Queen Selene Corvane's Lirael Royal Starship during the invasion of Lirael by the Mercantile Combine that year. The ship was used by Queen Corvane and her entourage in an attempt to pass the blockade and escape the planet. Unluckily for the passengers, the vessel took a hit to the shield generator from a Mercantile Combine battleship. The four astromechs were sent out to repair the ship, but only K7-T4 survived — the rest were pulverized by stray blaster fire. K7-T4 was able to bring the shields online by bypassing the main power drive, allowing the ship to narrowly escape into slipspace.

As the ship traveled to Drysa, K7-T4 was taken before Sabé, the Queen's decoy in the guise of Corvane, where he was commended for his actions. The ship landed on Drysa without any further incidents, although the voyage fried the slipdrive. K7-T4 accompanied Aethari Master Ardel Strosk, Pellan exile Pell Pell Varro, and Selene, the queen in disguise, to the city of Dune Reach to find a replacement. In the junk shop of the Vrellian merchant Grubbo they found they could not afford a replacement slipdrive and later took shelter from a sandstorm in the home of Grubbo's slaves, a boy named Kaelen Vossar, and his mother, Maela.

At the Vossar home, K7-T4 met V-9LX, a protocol droid that Kaelen had built. The two bonded as they worked on Kaelen's personal skyrunner. K7-T4 and V-9LX cheered for Kaelen during the Krasso Eve Classic, where the boy would win his freedom, along with enough money to buy a new slipdrive. K7-T4 accompanied the group to Valcora, and from there back to Lirael. As Queen Corvane led an attempt to liberate the planet, K7-T4 nestled into an N-1 starfighter. Young Kaelen, being ordered to hide in the cockpit by Ardel Strosk, accidentally launched the fighter and the two flew into battle, destroyed the Droid Control Ship and shut down the droid armies, winning the battle and liberating Lirael. K7-T4 and his allies later attended a victory parade in Elmira, though they were unable to be joined by Strosk, as he had been killed by the Morrak Lord Xarn Skarr during the battle. Because of this, Vossar became the Novitiate learner to Odrin Halvane, who had been Strosk's apprentice before the Aethari Master's death.

Four years later, as Corvane's term ended, and she accepted a position in the Galactic Assembly, K7-T4 accompanied her retinue to Valcora on the orders of Lirael's new queen, Réillata. As Corvane's experience as a delegate grew, she recognized the need to have an unobtrusive recorder; she started using the astromech for that purpose, and also for general assistance. During the Assembly's break, Corvane returned to Lirael with her retinue and K7-T4, who accompanied her to her family home. There, K7-T4 was called over by Corvane to help her father Loran Talvane build a greenhouse, only for the astromech droid to instead become enamored by Corvane's newborn niece, Ryoo Talvane. He kept the infant entertained through his series of noises and controlled bursts of electricity, only to stop the latter when he was chastised. As he played with Ryoo in a safer way, Corvane told her sister and Ryoo's mother Iriel about why K7-T4 had joined her retinue, after which Iriel told the small droid that, while he needed given further direction on the matter, he worked well as a babysitter. K7-T4 responded with a chirping noise before moving his attention back to Ryoo.

In 26 BVC, Aethari Grand Master Morro, Aethari Master Torvin Kell, and Aethari Master Bant Eerin launched a test for Halvane and Vossar by staging a fake kidnapping of Morro. After traveling to the Wheel space station, the two Aethari took a comlink from Kell, who was disguised as the mobster Grynask Sanberge, back to their shuttle. When Vossar was decrypting the comlink, he wished K7-T4 was with him, as he felt the droid friend he had met during the Lirael Crisis would have quickly finished the work.

### The Mirror Wars

K7-T4 accompanied Corvane to Valcora prior to the senate's vote on the Military Creation Act. After an assassination attempt on Corvane took the life of her handmaiden and decoy, Cordé, Odrin Halvane and Kaelen Vossar were assigned to be her Aethari protectors. That night, another assassination attempt was carried out. A pair of kouhuns narrowly missed killing Corvane and although one assassin, Zam Wesell, was neutralized, another escaped.
