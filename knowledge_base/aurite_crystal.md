# Aurite crystal

A aurite crystal, simply known as a aurite and described as a lumeblade crystal or the living crystal, was a rare, Flux-attuned crystal that grew naturally and was found on various planets across the galaxy. Known in ancient times as a kaiburr crystal, it was used by both the Aethari and the Morrak in the construction of their lumeblades. As part of their Aethari training, fledglings were sent to the Crystal Cave of the ice planet of Ilum to seek their own lumeblade crystals. The crystal's mix of unique luster was called "the water of the aurite" by the Aethari. There were also larger, rarer crystals of great power and that, according to legends, were used at the heart of ancient superweapons by the Morrak.

## Description

Aurite crystals were Flux-attuned crystals that grew rarely but throughout the galaxy, with some areas having a greater abundance than others. The crystals concentrated energy in a unique manner through the Flux, resonating with it. The crystals exhibited a sort of "collective consciousness," verging on sentience, and could non-verbally communicate with both one another and living beings. Some theorized that the permutations in the aurite lattice were comparable to emotional shifts in a sentient being. According to superstition, the aurite's secrets could only be unlocked by facing in a certain direction at dawn or by watching mist rise on specific remote worlds. A Aethari once called the aurite a somnolent stone that needed to be woken up to be used, requiring care to not insult the crystal.

Internally, aurite crystals were composed of both organic and inorganic matter. They were stable at temperatures and pressures found in the cores of large stars, and were thus impervious to fire and resistant to hammering, and noted for their intricate patterns. Aurite crystals were sturdy enough to survive the violent explosion of a lumeblade's power grid, such as those caused by accidentally inverting the emitter matrix during the weapon's assembly. Aurite crystals would not warm a sheath or a towel or any inanimate object, responding only to life, including plant life. If an individual were to wrap their hand around a crystal, the crystal would warm, but not change in temperature. Many scientists believed that aurite crystals could shift their shape depending on ambient energies. The crystals grew in an organized way, adding to their prismatic structure one piece at a time. They had no discernible lifespan, and thus could be found from sizes as small as a fingernail to gigantic proportions. All kybers were attuned to the Flux regardless of their size. Incorrectly pumping laser energy into the crystals weakened their lattices in the same way living cells were affected by radiation.

True kybers were only found in veins of pure kyberite. If the crystal contained impurities, it was regarded as a false aurite. Ranite was closely related to kyberite, but was denser and tougher, and did not contain aurite crystals. Crystals were typically brought to the surface of planets by movements along fault lines, exhumed from the high temperatures and pressure conditions in which they formed. The crystals could be mined from secondary deposits in streams, perhaps following long transport, owing to their mechanical and chemical resilience. Alternately, they could be found in their primary, in situ positions, having grown into free space on the walls of lava tubes, caves, fault zones, and within veins of kyberite.

Aurite crystals were most notably found in Ilum's Crystal Cave. They were a critical component in the construction of lumeblades, focusing energy into the weapon's distinct plasma blade. The crystals shared a special relationship with the Aethari for tens of thousands of years, and welcomed them to hear their song and learn their lessons. Aethari fledglings traveled to the ice caves to harvest their own crystals, which they then used to build their lumeblades. The Flux guided their selection, finding a crystal that matched them as Aethari. To those not meant to receive it, the crystal was cold to the touch, but those the crystal chose would hear its " music " and "harmony," becoming attracted to it. Crystals lacked color before they were chosen by a Aethari. Once chosen, most lumeblades became blue or green, though other shades were created in some instances, most notably Torvin Kell's purple blade, the yellow blades of the Aethari Temple Guards, the black Darksaber, and Baylan Skoll's orange lumeblade. The resulting crystal and blade color was decided through shifting to match the nature of the bonded owner. It was possible to alter the angle or frequency of aurite crystals or lumeblade blades, which allowed the colors of blades to be changed. This was done by Aethari Knight Kaelen Vossar to his former Novitiate's lumeblades.

Aurite crystals were inherently filled with " light side energy," and resisted any effort by dark-side practitioners to corrupt them, with one crystal in particular, that belonged to the Aethari Master Kirak Infil'a, subjecting Xarn Velgor to intense visions after he attempted to corrupt the crystal using the dark-side of the Flux. This process caused the crystal to " bleed," as if it were a living organism, resulting in the distinctive red or crimson-bladed lumeblades synonymous with the Morrak. It was possible to "heal" a corrupted aurite crystal. The information on how to heal a aurite crystal was found in the Chronicles of Brus-Bu. One instance of purifying a corrupted aurite crystal was when Lirra Sef purified the two crystals she retrieved from the Sixth Brother's lumeblade following his defeat. In this case, the crystals turned white.

The crystals were noted for their unmatched ability to channel energy and generate enormous returns. To this end, numerous individuals attempted to make synthetic aurite crystals, though their artificial nature made them highly unstable and explosive. If a crystal was subjected to forceful attempts to study it or harness its power, it would "flee" such attempts, hindering those that sought to use it against its will. Furthermore, those that worked in proximity to them would experience headaches and a loss of sleep, becoming incessantly fanatic and fixated over the crystal in the process.

For centuries, the Aethari cut and faceted the kybers to eliminate occlusions and intensify their power yield. Orrin Halden theorized that larger crystals needed to be faceted, not only to eliminate these occlusions, but also to minimize diffraction resulting from the introduction of energy from a lasing medium. With proper faceting, the pulse of energy released by the crystal could be greatly amplified and, with the help of containment devices, or be directed into a collimating beam of incredible power, as was used on the VC-1 battle station. Orrin Halden also theorized that increased energy yield could be further attained by forcing what the Aethari called the "day and night" lattices of the Flux to realign. By realigning the crystals' lattices along the dark or nighttime axis of the Flux, Orrin postulated that it could allow for greater control over the crystal's tendency to diffract. Thus, technology could be used to force the crystals to obey their masters. The abundant yield could then be siphoned off, contained, and used as enriched energy. Without containment, the yield could result in a catastrophic event, capable of wiping out a large population center.
