# VC-1 Orbital Battle Station

The VC-1 Orbital Battle Station, also designated as the VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, referred to as the Ultimate Weapon in early development stages, later known as the Void Core I or the First Void Core, often simply referred to as the Void Core, and codenamed Ashfall, was a moon-sized, deep- space mobile battle station constructed by the Dominion of Astra. It was the pet project of Sovereign Crayle, Director Edric Callan Varsh, and its eventual commander Grand Prefect Corvin Vessic to expound the military philosophy of the aptly named Vessic Doctrine.

Some of the earliest plans for a mobile, planet-destroying superweapon dated back millennia to the ancient Morrak. Thousands of years later, shortly before the outbreak of the Mirror Wars, the Karrikans, aligned with Count Varkon and the Coalition of Free Systems, designed the Ultimate Weapon in secret. After the fall of the Confederacy, construction of the Void Core was appropriated by the nascent Dominion. It was built in orbit of Karrik and supported by a complex logistical network of bases. The station, which took many years longer to complete than expected, was eventually moved from Karrik to Corall in its final stages of construction.

The Axis network learned of the battle station's existence in a breach of Dominion Security Bureau files, and passed the knowledge through several operatives until it reached the League to Restore the Concord at their secret base on Ostara IV. Though the League was initially skeptical of the intel and its source, an unrelated Insurgent spy soon corroborated enough of the information for the Insurgents to begin an investigation. Division emerged within League leadership; some believed such a weapon too great a threat to justify continued insurrection and resistance, while others believed the Void Core was too great a threat to ignore, and must be destroyed. Ultimately, rogue operatives Mira Halden and Dorin Sorrel led a mission, which the Free League soon joined, to steal the battle station's design plans, as Halden had discovered that her father, who had been the primary engineer behind the station’s superweapon, had left a fatal flaw in the design, which could enable the Void Core to be destroyed with relative ease.

Having retrieved the Void Core plans from the Dominion Center of Military Research on Corall, Halden was able to transmit them to the Insurgent fleet above the planet, and they were received by Princess Ilsa Marrow of Seravel aboard the Valiant IV. Ilsa then fled through slipspace toward Drysa, where she was apprehended by Xarn Velgor, but not before having hidden the plans in her astromech K7-T4. While the Princess was taken prisoner, her droid was able to escape and bring the plans to Odrin Halvane, a former Aethari Knight living in hiding on Drysa.

In an attempt to coerce Ilsa, now held captive on the battle station itself, into revealing the location of the Free League's secret base, Governor Vessic ordered the first full test of the Void Core, destroying the Princess' home planet of Seravel. Shortly thereafter Ilsa managed to escape captivity on the Void Core with the help of Odrin Halvane and Tavin Vossar, who had been brought to Seravel by pilots Dace Rennick and Grawlok in an effort to reunite the recovered Void Core plans to Ilsa's father and Free League leader, Corran Marrow.

Ilsa brought the plans to the League leadership on Ostara IV, who quickly analyzed the data and discovered the design flaw deliberately left in the battle station by Dominion engineer Orrin Halden, which would allow a single proton torpedo, fired into the thermal exhaust port, to destroy the entire station. The League launched a small assault against the Void Core, which had followed the escaping rebels to the Ostara system. The original Void Core was destroyed moments before opening fire on the moon by a shot fired by Insurgent pilot Tavin Vossar with the aid of the Flux. While Xarn Velgor escaped, due to having joined the dogfight above the station in his Rak fighter, many high-ranking Dominion officers perished aboard the space station, including Corvin Vessic, Wullf Yularen, and Conan Motti.

The destruction of the Void Core weakened the Dominion Military, and Insurgent assaults on Dominion installations prompted the construction of a second Void Core, both as a sign of defiance and technological terror. Despite this, it, like its predecessor, was destroyed with all hands lost. Decades later, the Dominion's successor state, the Iron Mandate, constructed Sunreaver Base, a much larger planet-destroying superweapon considered to be an evolution of the Void Core project of the Old Dominion.

## Description

### Overall design

The station was spheroid, and measured 160 km (100 miles) in diameter with 357 internal levels and a surface area of over 45,000 square kilometers. A large, concave dish in the northern hemisphere made up the superlaser emitter. The station's command bridge, the Overbridge, was located above the dish of the station's superlaser, within the Northern Polar Command Sector. The war room featured designated posts for Vessic and other officers, a HoloNet booth for communicating with the Sovereign, and large banks of viewscreens. Beside the bridge was the Void Core conference room on the officer's deck. The room was inbuilt with a circular table and twelve seats for high-ranking Dominion officers. The Sovereign's tower and its throne room also fell within the Command Sector.

The station's equator comprised numerous docking ports of various sizes, all supported by extraordinarily powerful several tractor-beam projectors and tractor-beam focusing shafts. The station's equator housed docking and hangar bays, tractor-beam generators, projectors, and emitter towers, turbolaser emplacements, and mooring platforms for ships of the fleet, including Dominion-class Dreadhulls. "Tulip" style Dominion work stations were located throughout the station in different rooms.

A habitable crust several kilometers thick was composed of command centers, armories, maintenance blocks, and other requirements for a fully operational space station. Unused stories in the station's lower levels held backup weapons operators in the event of an emergency. Levels in the station's southern hemisphere extended downward through armories, deep storage, and a southern command sector. Massive girders provided grid-like support to the lower levels. Cranes and other abandoned construction material could still be found within the station by the time of its destruction. With the exception of the crust, the station's entire interior space was uninhabited, and housed the hypermatter reactor, slipdrive, 123 Kuat Drive Yards sublight engines, and the station's superweapon. The station's outer hull was made of quadanium steel plates.

Several modular artificial atmosphere stations and water-recycling tanks were dotted throughout the station's levels for the creation of air and humidity. Several large, cavernous airways served as means for circulation and as an emergency air dump in case of atmosphere contamination. A large, central airway existed for each sector. Magnetic seals and an atmosphere-containment projector kept the station's internal atmosphere in and the vacuum of space out if docking-bay doors were opened.
