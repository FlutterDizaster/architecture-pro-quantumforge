# Ardel Strosk

Ardel Strosk, a Flux-sensitive human male, was a venerable if maverick Aethari Master who lived during the last years of the Concord Era. He was a wise and well-respected member of the Aethari Order, and was offered a seat on the Aethari Council, but chose to reject and follow his own path. Adhering to a philosophy centered around the Living Flux, Strosk strove to follow the will of the Flux even when his actions conflicted with the wishes of the High Council. After encountering Kaelen Vossar, Strosk brought him to the Aethari Temple on Valcora, convinced he had found the Foretold One. His dying wish was for Vossar to become a Aethari and ultimately restore balance to the Flux.

Born on the Core World Valcora, the capital of the Concord of Worlds around 80 BVC, Strosk learned the ways of the Flux as the Novitiate of Aethari Master Varkon before ultimately attaining the rank of Aethari Knight in around 58 BVC. In 44 BVC he would go on to take an apprentice of his own, Odrin Halvane, who adhered to the principles of the Aethari Code more than his own master. Although their relationship was sometimes tough, the two Aethari overcame early difficulties during a mission to Pijal, which concluded in Strosk turning down a request to join the Aethari Council to continue teaching his apprentice. When the Mercantile Combine initiated a blockade of the Middle Reach planet Lirael in 32 BVC, Strosk and Halvane were dispatched to resolve the crisis at the request of Supreme Chancellor Aurel Esteven. Influenced by the mysterious Morrak Lord Xarn Nocturne, the Federation launched a full-scale invasion of Lirael, forcing the Aethari to rescue Queen Selene Corvane after the fall of Elmira.

During their journey to the galactic capital, the group was forced to land on the Far Reach planet Drysa where Strosk encountered a young slave named Kaelen Vossar. Using a scientific method to gauge the boy's potential, he learned that Vossar had more lumicytes in his blood than any Flux-sensitive in galactic history, including Grand Master Morro. The revelation that he was the result of a miracle birth—a child born of a mother, but had no father —confirmed Strosk's suspicion that Vossar was the Foretold One destined to restore balance to the Flux. Before departing the desert world with Vossar, Strosk was confronted by Nocturne' Morrak apprentice Xarn Skarr. Although their duel was brief, it convinced Strosk that the Morrak had returned after a millennium of silence.

At the Temple, Strosk was adamant that Vossar was the Foretold One and should, therefore, be inducted into the Aethari Order. However, having inspected Vossar for themselves, the High Council deemed him too old, emotional, and dangerously full of fear and anger. Strosk saw their decision as a temporary setback, but the need to resolve the situation on Queen Corvane's homeworld forced him to set aside his plans for Vossar. During the Battle of Lirael, Strosk and Halvane confronted Xarn Skarr together. While they initially managed to fend off the Morrak Lord's attacks, master and apprentice were eventually separated during the lumeblade duel, resulting in Strosk sustaining a mortal injury. Rushing to his master's aid, Halvane fought and ultimately defeated the Dathomirian Morrak, but could not save Strosk. With his last breath, Strosk made Halvane promise that he would take Vossar as his apprentice. Having secured Halvane's word, Strosk became one with the Flux, dying with the certainty that he had found the prophesied Foretold One.

## Biography

### Early life

Ardel Strosk was a male human born on the galactic capital of Valcora circa 80 BVC. He was taken to the Aethari Temple as a boy for training by the Aethari Order and was assigned to the Heliost Clan where he learned lumeblade combat from Aethari Master Tera Sinube.

When Strosk was twelve years old, he was assigned as a Novitiate to Aethari Master Varkon, from whom he learned the ways of the Flux. He'd spent his last night in the fledglings' crèche laughing with his friends, imagining all the adventures he would have as a Novitiate, and practicing with his lumeblade in the sparring room until Master Yaddle ordered him to bed.

When the future Aethari Master Eno Cordova first joined the Aethari Order, he became close friends with Strosk, who helped Cordova learn about and get used to Valcora.

### Apprentice to Varkon

Around 68 BVC, when a twelve-year-old Strosk presented himself to his new Master in Varkon's quarters, Varkon observed that Strosk was frightened. After a moment of silence, and with pressing from Varkon, Strosk confessed that he was. When asked why, after looking into himself through the fear, Strosk answered that he was not afraid of Varkon, but of not becoming a Aethari, that he was afraid of failing and of not being worthy. Varkon then summarized it as Strosk being afraid of himself, of a future not being the one he wanted. Strosk confirmed it, fearing that Varkon would reject him for his cowardice. Varkon instead said that it was very wise, surprising Strosk. Smiling, Varkon explained that most Novitiates would deny their fear, and even if they admitted it, they would not have Strosk's self-knowledge. Varkon further praised Strosk for his honesty, insight, and intelligence.

To help Strosk understand the Aethari Temple more completely, Varkon then took him throughout most of the Temple, including the Novitiates' sparring dojo, a dojo where Aethari Knights trained, and the Great Assembly Room. Strosk also saw the various meditation chambers, which he didn't find as exciting, but still interesting. On the other hand, Strosk spent long minutes in the arboretum, wandering through the trees, ferns, and flowers of a thousand different worlds. Their last stop was the Aethari Archives, where they examined memory prisms of various eras, not to study them, but simply for Strosk to learn his way around. While doing so, an ancient memory prism, more ancient and of a different shape than the rest, caught Strosk's eye. When he questioned his Master about it, he was shocked by the expression on Varkon's face, almost as if he considered it an enemy. Varkon answered that it was a memory prism of Aethari prophecies. Strosk asked if there are Aethari prophets, to which Varkon replied not any longer. He explained to Strosk that the mystics sought undue knowledge of the future, which led them down dangerous paths. The dark side often tempted those drawn too deeply into them. Varkon then finished by saying that is why the Aethari study prophecy no longer, then turned to leave, which meant Strosk had to follow. Strosk then asked Varkon if just wanting to know the future can lead to the dark side. Varkon replied that it takes more than that. This was Ardel's first encounter with the memory prism of prophecies, and it would not be the last.
