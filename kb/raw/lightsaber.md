# Lightsaber

The lightsaber, also referred to as a laser sword by those who were unfamiliar with it, was a weapon usually used by the Jedi, Sith, and other Force-sensitives. Lightsabers had a plasma blade emitted from a usually metal hilt, and could be shut off at will or at the touch of a button. The hilt contained a power cell, like the Diatium power cell in Darth Vader's lightsaber. It also contained a kyber crystal which had been attuned to the Force by a Jedi, and which amplified the energy from the power cell to create the plasma beam, as well as containing it within a blade-like field. The energy blade had no mass, but lightsabers operated on principles of controlled electromagnetic arc-wave energy, creating a gyroscopic effect that made them challenging to handle. It was a weapon that required skill and training, and was greatly enhanced when used in conjunction with the Force. Though also used by the Sith, the lightsaber was synonymous with the Jedi, with some in the galaxy believing only Jedi could use lightsabers.

Lightsabers were generally used for both offense and defense. A lightsaber could cut through virtually anything, from flesh to blast doors. The only ways to block the incoming attack of a lightsaber was with a weapon made with material that conducted energy, such as an electrostaff and a Z6 riot control baton, resistant metals like beskar and cortosis, another lightsaber or an ichor sword. When used defensively, a Force-sensitive could deflect blaster bolts with a lightsaber, and with skill, could even reflect the shots back toward the shooter or some other target. Experienced Jedi could even employ their lightsabers to absorb Force lightning. Most practitioners used one single-bladed lightsaber, though some used double-bladed lightsabers or even multiple lightsabers at once. Despite lacking Force-sensitivity, the cyborg General Grievous was capable of wielding four lightsabers at once thanks to his advanced mechanical body and enhanced cybernetic brain.

Lightsaber combat was divided into seven Forms: Form I, Form II, Form III, Form IV, Form V, Form VI, and Form VII.

## History

### Early history

Sounding like no other weapon in the galaxy, lightsabers were used thousands of years before the age of the Galactic Republic, seeing use in the Great Scourge of Malachor. They were also used in the Battles of Rashfond and the Peacekeeping of Parliock, The proto-saber was an ancient type of prototype lightsaber.

### Republic Era

During the history of the Jedi Order, the lightsaber was considered to be the Jedi's only true ally in battle. As Master Obi-Wan Kenobi once declared to his Padawan Anakin Skywalker in the days of the late Republic, the lightsaber was a Jedi's life. Jedi saw the lightsaber as a symbol of the care that they would wield using their gifts given by the Force, and also served as a message to their opponents: mastering the lightsaber required great skill and training. The choice of the saber was also the symbol of their role and duty as protectors, not destroyers, and the fact that the Jedi did not seek power and only sought to stand tall against darkness. Still, Jedi knew they would need to be more than their lightsabers.

When Padawan Enya Keen lost her lightsaber during a mission to Dalna, her master, Char-Ryl-Roy, reminded her that she would need to act as a Jedi without the weapon. While finding a kyber crystal was still a sacred part of a Jedi's duty and Keen felt like losing her lightsaber would feel like losing part of herself, she willingly gave away the crystal if it meant finding more about the Path of the Open Hand and its plans. During the sabotage of the Steady Wing in 232 BBY, Padawan Imri Cantaros dealt with emotional struggles that lead to his lightsaber beam turning transparent and weak. Lightsabers took on a more prominent role in galactic affairs as the Clone Wars came to pass. Jedi, previously peacekeepers, became Jedi Generals of the Grand Army of the Republic, and thus began using their lightsabers more often than they had in their role of peacekeeping. Despite his lack of Force-sensitivity and formal Jedi training, General Grievous received instruction in lightsaber combat from Count Dooku. His cybernetic arms could split into four separate appendages, allowing him to wield up to four lightsabers simultaneously. Grievous frequently hunted Jedi individually, claiming their lightsabers as trophies. By the end of the Clone Wars, Grievous had amassed a collection of nearly a hundred lightsabers, many reportedly taken from Jedi he had captured or killed, including Jedi Master Neebo and the newly knighted Nahdar Vebb.

### Imperial Era

For the majority of the Empire's reign, the only lightsabers that were prominently used were those of the Sith Lord and Imperial enforcer Darth Vader and the Inquisitors who served him, with the Inquisitors eventually vanishing alongside the Jedi they had hunted. For a time, Vader had remained largely hidden from the galaxy, leaving the Inquisitors to—as noted by the later historian Beaumont Kin —possibly be more well-known lightsaber wielders. Eventually, however, Vader became a publicly known and widely feared figure.

The majority of the Jedi Order, meanwhile, had been reduced to scattered remnants in hiding across the galaxy who largely kept their lightsabers hidden, lest they draw Imperial attention. The Sith Lord Darth Sidious, as the emperor of the galaxy, largely ruled from a distance and was unable to use his own lightsabers in combat often. Vader wielded a dual-phase lightsaber, a design of an older, more dangerous generation. With the disappearance of the Inquisitors and through his own public emergence over the course of the Imperial Era, Vader became the primary example of someone in the galaxy who fought with a lightsaber.

However, other than Vader as he became an increasingly known figure, the general public saw little of lightsabers during the Imperial Era. Indeed, surviving Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi regarded the lightsaber as a past weapon created in "a more civilized age." Senator Daho Sejan had hope that general knowledge of the lightsaber would die off in due time, leaving only Imperial narratives about the fall of the Jedi to be told whenever a child asked what it was. During the Galactic Civil War, Domina Tagge of the Tagge Corporation tried to recreate the lightsaber through the Tagge protoblade.

All the same, there were still lightsabers used by Jedi survivors and those they trained, like Obi-Wan Kenobi, Luke Skywalker, Cere Junda, Cal Kestis, Tensu Run, Ahsoka Tano, Kanan Jarrus, and Ezra Bridger. While Kenobi, for a period, kept his lightsaber absolutely hidden, he was inspired to take back up his weapon and found his hope for the future rekindled through his experiences with the young Luke Skywalker and Leia Organa, resulting in him effectively using his weapon against Vader during a duel on a barren moon in 9 BBY. Meanwhile, Kestis had been using his lightsaber as part of his personal crusade against the Empire, through which he became a well-known criminal. All the while in the Great Jedi Purge, numerous surviving Jedi were wiped out by Vader and his Inquisitors.
